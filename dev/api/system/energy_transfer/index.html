Energy transfer between the distributions

src/system/energy_transfer_terms.jl

Exported

Runes2P.InterfaceEnergyFluxTerm — Type
InterfaceEnergyFluxTerm(interface::MomentumInterfaceTerm; coefficient=-1)

coefficient times the kinetic energy that electrons carry through the momentum interface from the lower grid to the upper one, per volume and second [W/m³]: mₑc²(γ − 1) of the last momentum cell of the lower grid times interface_flux. The interface takes the electrons from that cell, so this is the energy that the discrete energy moment of the lower grid loses (the energy at the interface itself differs by about Δp/(2p)). With the default coefficient = −1 in the row of the temperature it is the energy that leaves the total energy of the electrons on the hot-tail grid for the runaway grid (total_energy_equation), so the electrons that cross take their energy with them and do not heat the cold ones.

Runes2P.KineticEnergyLossTerm — Type
KineticEnergyLossTerm(source::FokkerPlanckTerm; part=:free_electrons, region=nothing,
                      coefficient=part === :free_electrons ? 1 : -1)

coefficient times the power [W/m³] that the electrons of the kinetic unknown of source lose through a part of their collisions, for the row of the temperature of the cold electrons:

\[L = −\frac{1}{V'} \sum_{ij} w_i\, m_e c^2 (γ_i − 1)\, [C_a f − C_b f]_{ij}\, 𝒱'_{ij} Δp_i Δξ_j,\]

with the envelope w of region (1 without one, MomentumRegion) and the operators of the plasma, collision settings, scheme and boundaries of source:

  • part = :free_electrons: C_a the electron-electron part (electron_electron_operator), C_b = 0: the energy that the electrons give to the free cold electrons, the friction and, in the fully kinetic mode, the energy diffusion. For a superthermal electron it is DREAM's ∫ v F_s f of CollisionalEnergyTransferKineticTerm. With coefficient = 1 it is the channel of the runaway grid into the total energy of the electrons on the hot-tail grid (total_energy_equation).
  • part = :sinks: C_a the collisions of source without the field, C_b their electron-electron part: the energy that leaves the electrons by the friction on bound electrons (excitation and ionization), bremsstrahlung and synchrotron radiation. With coefficient = −1 and the hot region of the total energy as region it is the loss of that total, so the cold electrons receive exactly the free-electron part of what the hot electrons lose. Both operators are applied, since the weights of exponential fitting depend on all coefficients of an operator. With bound_friction = :ionization_split of CollisionSettings the friction on bound electrons holds the energy of the kinetic ionization (KineticIonizationTerm) up to the discretization and the width of the threshold (see there): the ionization potentials, which the ions store and release at recombination, and the energy of the secondaries, which SecondaryHeatingTerm gives back to the cold electrons (ionization_energy_flows). With DREAM's :bethe it holds that energy only on average and only where the screening window is open: from the ionization potential up to the mean excitation energy it takes none.

The term takes the plasma of source in the same treatment: implicit, with the columns of the temperature and density unknowns, or lagged at the state of the start of the step. A region whose limit follows the temperature adds the column of its envelope.

Runes2P.FreeElectronEnergyTransferTerm — Method
FreeElectronEnergyTransferTerm(source::FokkerPlanckTerm)

The power that the electrons of the unknown of source give to the free cold electrons by collisions: KineticEnergyLossTerm with part = :free_electrons, the transfer from the runaway grid to the cold electrons.
