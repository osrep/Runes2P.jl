Runes2P.EnergyMomentTerm — Type
EnergyMomentTerm(kinetic, grid; coefficient=1, region=nothing)

coefficient times the kinetic energy density W(r) = (1/V') Σ 𝒱' Δp Δξ mₑc²(γ − 1) f [J/m³ for f in m⁻³] of the kinetic unknown kinetic on grid, added to a radial row unknown with one component, with γ at the momentum cell centres (DREAM's KineticEnergyFromDistributionFunction). With region, a MomentumRegion, the moment covers that part of the grid, as DensityMomentTerm: the energy of the hot electrons above the threshold, whose limit may follow the temperature unknown. Linear in the distribution; the column of the temperature is that of the envelope.

The term keeps the positions of its row in the stored Jacobian between calls, so its add_jacobian! is not safe to call concurrently.

Runes2P.RunawayFluidEnergyTransferTerm — Type
RunawayFluidEnergyTransferTerm(plasma; runaway_density=:runaway_density,
                               temperature=:cold_electron_temperature,
                               density=:cold_electron_density,
                               coulomb_logarithm=:energy_dependent, ions=nothing)

DREAM's heating of the cold electrons by the friction of fluid runaways when there is no runaway grid (include/DREAM/Equations/Fluid/CollisionalEnergyTransferREFluidTerm.hpp, the momentum 20 at line 26, used in src/Settings/Equations/T_cold.cpp), in W/m³ for the row of the temperature:

\[e c E_c n_\mathrm{re} = 4π r_0^2 m_e c^3 \ln Λ_{ee}(p = 20)\, n_\mathrm{cold} n_\mathrm{re},\]

the friction at the speed of light on runaways of momentum 20 mₑc. The Coulomb logarithm of electrons takes the temperature unknown and the free-electron density of the prescribed ions in plasma (a PlasmaState or a function of time), or with ions of that radial unknown of the charge states; coulomb_logarithm is the model of coulomb_logarithm. DREAM evaluates the logarithm with the model of its collision settings, whose code default is the thermal one, independent of p; the default :energy_dependent here is the setting of DREAM's disruption example (examples/Disruptions/generate.py:145). The term is the DREAM-compatible mode of the energy equation.

Runes2P.thermal_energy_equation — Method
thermal_energy_equation(; energy=:cold_thermal_energy, temperature=:cold_electron_temperature,
                        density=:cold_electron_density)

The algebraic equation of the energy of the cold electrons, 0 = 3/2 e n T − W with W in J/m³ and T in eV (DREAM's W_cold). The row of the temperature holds the time derivative of the energy, Equation(temperature, TimeDerivative(energy), ...), with the heating and loss terms in W/m³, as in DREAM, where the energy is the unknown of the time derivative and the temperature that of its own row.

The energy takes its initial value from the row (initial_value = :from_equation): integrate sets it from the temperature and the density of the initial state, and the value of the energy in that state is not read.

Runes2P.total_energy_equation — Method
total_energy_equation(kinetic, grid; region, total_energy=:total_electron_energy,
                      energy=:cold_thermal_energy)

The algebraic equation of the total energy W_tot [J/m³] of the electrons in the fully kinetic mode:

\[W_\mathrm{tot} = W_\mathrm{cold} + \sum_{p > p_0} m_e c^2 (γ − 1) f\, ΔV,\]

the energy energy of the cold electrons and that of the kinetic unknown kinetic on grid in the hot region region (EnergyMomentTerm), the region of the hot density. The row of the temperature then holds the time derivative of total_energy (TimeDerivative(total_energy)) with the channels that leave or enter the electrons: the work of the field on all of them (E j_ohm + E j_hot in the fully kinetic mode, where j_ohm = j(f) − j_hot + E(σ − σ_num) already leaves out the hot current, so the two products count each electron once), the radiated power of the ions, the heat transport and the exchange with the ions. The collisional transfer from the hot electrons to the cold ones, the electrons that cross the threshold, and the threshold that moves with the temperature are then exact for any time stepper, since the time derivative acts on the energy of the hot region itself. DREAM's fully kinetic mode has none of them (T_cold.cpp:313-321).

Every other change of the energy of the hot region is charged to the cold electrons, since W_cold = W_tot − W_hot. The row must therefore list each term of the equation of kinetic that moves energy out of the electrons or into them: KineticEnergyLossTerm with part = :sinks for the bremsstrahlung, the synchrotron loss and the friction on bound electrons, MomentumBoundaryFluxTerm(...; weight = :energy) for an outflow at p_max of each Fokker-Planck term with collisions or with the field as an unknown, InterfaceEnergyFluxTerm and InterfaceCollisionWorkTerm of the same interface for the runaway grid, and KineticEnergyChangeTerm of each RosenbluthPutvinskiSourceTerm for the energy of the knock-ons of the fluid runaways above the grid, over the whole grid. Each FokkerPlanckTerm of kinetic whose operator holds nonzero radial coefficients needs a KineticEnergyChangeTerm of that term without a region, for the change of the energy of all of kinetic by the transport at each radius. The run requires it before its first step for a RechesterRosenbluthUpdate that sets the transport. The system refuses a row that misses one of these terms. It refuses two energy terms of one source. It refuses a HeatTransportTerm with the Rechester-Rosenbluth diffusivity next to the energy term of a radial transport, which already moves the heat of the bulk. It refuses a KineticEnergyChangeTerm whose source is not in the equation of a distribution in the total energy, since the energy that it counts is outside the total. It refuses an InterfaceCollisionWorkTerm of an interface whose lower distribution, that of the grid below the face, is not in the total energy, since the electrons that cross there are outside the total. A second InterfaceCollisionWorkTerm of one interface, which would count the work twice, is refused in a row of any kind. The system refuses the transfer of kinetic to the cold electrons (KineticEnergyLossTerm with part = :free_electrons, FrictionEnergyTransferTerm), which stays inside the total. The system refuses the sinks term and the energy flux through p_max of a distribution outside the total, which loses that energy itself. When the system is built, it refuses a SecondaryHeatingTerm whose kinetic ionization takes another region than the energy moment, since the heating would then not match the ionizations that the sinks charge. The system also refuses the terms of the equation of kinetic for which the system has no energy term, since the cold electrons would pay or gain their energy at the radius where the electrons are moved, created or removed:

  • Knock-on secondaries on its grid (KnockOnTerm) are refused.
  • A FokkerPlanckTerm without collisions, with no plasma and no coefficient source, whose operator holds coefficients in momentum is refused: no term of the row carries the energy that they move. The coefficients of a unit field, those of the field term at 1 V/m that a term with the field unknown multiplies with E (set_unit_field_coefficients!), are not among them.
  • A Fokker-Planck term without collisions that carries the field unknown is refused in a distribution with an interface to a runaway grid, whatever its own p_upper_boundary: put the field on the Fokker-Planck term with collisions at the interface.
  • A nonzero prescribed flux through p_min of a Fokker-Planck term with collisions is refused. A zero flux passes.
  • A prescribed loss rate (LossRateTerm) and a Maxwellian source (MaxwellianSourceTerm) are refused.
  • A LinearTerm with a nonzero coefficient in the time derivative of kinetic, which moves electrons into kinetic from a kinetic unknown or out of it, is refused.

The checks read the coefficients of the operators as they are, and a run repeats them before its first step, except the region of the heating by secondaries.

A ParticleSourceTerm is accepted. With the constraint 0 = n(f) − n_cold − n_hot for its magnitude, it moves electrons between the cold population and kinetic, so the cold electrons rightly pay the energy that its electrons bring into the hot region. A Maxwellian source adds or removes electrons from outside the system, so no population of the system should pay or gain their energy.

The sinks and the transfer to the free electrons (KineticEnergyLossTerm) take no flux through p_max, whose energy is the boundary term's alone. The sinks term is required by the settings of the collisions (synchrotron, bremsstrahlung, partial or no screening on), not by its value: at B = 0 a synchrotron loss of zero still needs the term, so that the row stays closed when the field of the plasma changes.

total_energy takes its initial value from the row, after the energy of the cold electrons.

Runes2P.FrictionEnergyTransferTerm — Type
FrictionEnergyTransferTerm(kinetic, grid, plasma; settings=FRICTION_TRANSFER_SETTINGS,
                           coefficient=1)

coefficient times the power [W/m³] that the electrons of the kinetic unknown kinetic on grid give to the free cold electrons by friction, for the row of the temperature of the cold electrons, as the moment

\[P = \frac{1}{V'} \sum_{ij} m_e c\, v_i\, p_i\, ν_s(p_i)\, f_{ij}\, 𝒱'_{ij} Δp_i Δξ_j,\]

with v and p at the momentum cell centres and the slowing-down frequency ν_s (slowing_down_frequency) of settings and of plasma, a PlasmaState, a function of time that returns one, or a PlasmaSource such as PlasmaFromUnknowns. This is DREAM's CollisionalEnergyTransferKineticTerm (src/Equations/Fluid/CollisionalEnergyTransferKineticTerm.cpp, DREAM v26.5), the heating of the cold electrons by f_hot in a run without the full collision frequencies (src/Settings/Equations/T_cold.cpp:318-340) and by f_re in every run (lines 341-354), for which free_electron_energy_transfer_term is Runes2P's default. The default settings, FRICTION_TRANSFER_SETTINGS, are those DREAM fixes for it: the superthermal frequencies with complete screening, so that the electrons collide with the cold electrons alone, the energy-dependent Coulomb logarithm and no bremsstrahlung.

The moment reads the distribution at the cell centres, so it holds whatever scheme the operator of f takes, and it counts the energy of an electron until it leaves the grid, also through p = 0 where the friction of the superthermal frequencies carries it out under p_zero_boundary = :outflow (compare KineticEnergyLossTerm, the energy change of the electron-electron operator itself). The term is linear in f. With a plasma of unknowns its Jacobian holds the columns of the temperature, the density and, when the plasma reads them, the ion charge states, as DREAM's. The weights of the moment are kept until the plasma values change. A PlasmaState given as plasma is read once, so a change made to it in place afterwards is not seen.

The row of a total energy (total_energy_equation) refuses the term for a distribution in that total, since the transfer stays inside it.

Runes2P.InterfaceCollisionWorkTerm — Type
InterfaceCollisionWorkTerm(interface::MomentumInterfaceTerm; coefficient=-1)

coefficient times the work [W/m³] that the electron-electron collisions do on the electrons that cross the momentum interface, in a radial row: the part Γ_ee [m⁻³ s⁻¹] of interface_flux that the collisions with the free electrons drive, times the energy step between the first momentum cell of the upper grid and the last of the lower grid,

\[P = Γ_{ee} \left[E_k(p_{\mathrm{up},1}) − E_k(p_{\mathrm{low},N})\right], \quad E_k(p) = m_e c^2 (γ − 1).\]

Γ_ee is positive from the lower grid to the upper one. The step is positive, so P is positive where the collisions push electrons up through the face (the energy diffusion) and negative where they pull them down (the friction). An electron that crosses leaves the lower grid with the energy of its last cell and enters the upper grid with the energy of its first cell (InterfaceEnergyFluxTerm).

No operator holds the step, since neither grid takes a flux through the face in its energy moments (KineticEnergyLossTerm). The cold electrons pay it for Γ_ee. The sinks pay it for the friction on bound electrons, the energy diffusion by bound electrons under partial screening, bremsstrahlung and synchrotron radiation. The row of a total energy credits the cold electrons with the part of the step of the field up to the face, which the runaway grid receives as well (see the section The energy step at the interface of the docs page Energy and current balance).

With the default coefficient = −1 in the row of the temperature the cold electrons pay the work of the electron-electron collisions, as they gain the energy that these collisions take from the electrons on either grid. Charging that work to the cold electrons is a choice of this package (see the docs page Choices of this package). In every row the system refuses a second InterfaceCollisionWorkTerm of one interface, which would count the work twice. In the row of a total energy (total_energy_equation) it requires one such term next to the InterfaceEnergyFluxTerm of the same interface, with a negative coefficient. It refuses there one of an interface whose lower distribution, that of the grid below the face, is not in the total energy. A row of the cold thermal energy alone (thermal_energy_equation) takes the term without these two conditions, the requirement next to the interface energy term and the refusal of a distribution outside the total. It is optional there. With coefficient = −1 it adds the work that the moments of free_electron_energy_transfer_term of the two grids leave out, where the row holds that term of both grids. A test sums these channels of the two grids by hand, without such a row, and the sum closes with the work.

Each overlap cell of the face carries the flux Γ = A f_face − D (f_up − f_low)/Δp. A and D are the coefficients of the lower operator at the face, the field included, and Δp is the distance of the two cell centres. The face value f_face is that of the scheme of the interface, which the whole coefficients set: the upwind side of A, the linear interpolation of :central, or, for exponential fitting, the weight (B(−w) − 1)/w of the lower cell with B(x) = x/(eˣ − 1) and w = A Δp/D. With pitch_mapping = :dream it is the upwind side of A against the upper value interpolated in the pitch.

Γ_ee takes the same face value with the coefficients of the electron-electron collisions alone, A_ee = −p ν_s,ee [mₑc/s] and, in mode = :full, D_ee = Θ γ ν_s,ee [(mₑc)²/s]. Here p is the momentum of the face [mₑc], ν_s,ee [s⁻¹] the slowing-down frequency on the free electrons and Θ = T/(mₑc²), as in electron_electron_operator. The parts of the field, of these collisions and of the sinks therefore add up to the flux of the interface.

The term reads the plasma and the settings of the record of the coefficients of the lower operator at each evaluation, so a lagged plasma or lagged ions of the Fokker-Planck term are taken as the flux takes them. It compares the values of that plasma, not the object. So it agrees with the operator whenever the operator was rebuilt after the last change of that plasma, as in a run. It keeps the coefficients of the face. It rebuilds them when the Fokker-Planck term of the interface rebuilds its coefficients, or when those plasma values or settings differ from the kept ones.

An operator not yet built from a plasma, such as that of a lagged term before its first prepare_step!, holds no collisions, and the term is zero there. The settings take mode = :full or :superthermal.

The row reads the cells of both grids at the face. It reads the plasma columns of the Fokker-Planck term when its coefficients follow the plasma unknowns (has_plasma_columns). It reads the column of the field only with exponential fitting and pitch_mapping = :overlap, where the weight of the face follows the field. It reads none when a Fokker-Planck term with a plasma and mode = :superthermal holds the coefficients, since the energy diffusion is zero there and the face takes the upwind side. The term assembles the row itself (jacobian_assembly).

Runes2P.InterfaceEnergyFluxTerm — Type
InterfaceEnergyFluxTerm(interface::MomentumInterfaceTerm; coefficient=-1)

coefficient times the kinetic energy that electrons carry through the momentum interface from the lower grid to the upper one, per volume and second [W/m³]: mₑc²(γ − 1) of the last momentum cell of the lower grid times interface_flux. The interface takes the electrons from that cell, so this is the energy that the discrete energy moment of the lower grid loses (the energy at the interface itself differs by about Δp/(2p)). With the default coefficient = −1 in the row of the temperature it is the energy that leaves the total energy of the electrons on the hot-tail grid for the runaway grid (total_energy_equation), so the electrons that cross take their energy with them and do not heat the cold ones. The energy that they gain or lose between the two cells at the face is not in this term: the part of the electron-electron collisions is InterfaceCollisionWorkTerm. When the flux follows the plasma unknowns of the Fokker-Planck term of the interface, the row has their columns (has_plasma_columns). The row reads the cells of both grids at the face, so the term assembles it itself (jacobian_assembly) and the coloring of the automatic Jacobian does not see it.

Runes2P.KineticEnergyChangeTerm — Type
KineticEnergyChangeTerm(source; region=nothing, coefficient=1)

coefficient times the kinetic energy that the term source gives the electrons of its distribution per volume and second [W/m³], for a radial row with one component: the energy moment of the rate S_ij [m⁻³ s⁻¹] that source adds to the cell (i, j),

\[P = \frac{1}{V'} \sum_{ij} w_i\, m_e c^2 (γ_i − 1)\, S_{ij}\, 𝒱'_{ij} Δp_i Δξ_j,\]

with the envelope w of region (1 without one, MomentumRegion). source is a RosenbluthPutvinskiSourceTerm or a FokkerPlanckTerm whose operator declares radial transport (below), and another source throws an ArgumentError.

The rate of a RosenbluthPutvinskiSourceTerm is bilinear in the runaway density n_re and the total density n_tot [m⁻³], so the power is P = coefficient n_re n_tot e^± at each radius, with

\[e^\pm = \sum_i w_i\, m_e c^2 (γ_i − 1)\, R_i \quad [\mathrm{J\, m^3/s}],\]

the sum over the momentum cells i whose pitch cells hold ±ξ*, and R_i the rate rosenbluth_putvinski_rate of the cell above the cutoff of the source. The term computes e⁺ and e⁻ per radius from the shapes of source when it is built, so it counts exactly the cells that the source fills. It takes e⁺ or e⁻ by the sign of the electric field of the source, e⁺ without one. The Jacobian holds the columns of n_re and n_tot, and the sign of the field enters without a derivative, as in the source.

The limit of the region of this source is fixed: a number or one number per radius, in units of mₑc. A limit that follows the temperature or another function of time throws an ArgumentError.

In the row of the temperature under total_energy_equation the system requires this term for each Rosenbluth-Putvinski source of a distribution of the total energy, with that source of the system itself and no region, since every knock-on is in the total energy. The system checks the sign of the coefficient, and a coefficient of 1 closes the budget. It refuses the term there when its source is not in the equation of such a distribution. A row of the cold thermal energy alone (thermal_energy_equation) holds no distribution, and the system refuses the term there. The term creates the energy of the knock-ons in the system, a choice of this package (see the docs page Choices of this package): their primaries, the fluid runaways above the grid, have no energy unknown, so no population pays it. The alternative, which the package rejects, charges that energy to the cold electrons.

Next to RunawayFluidEnergyTransferTerm in the same row, the total energy gains the energy of the knock-ons twice, since the stopping power e c E_c of that heating includes the collisions that create them (see the docs page Energy and current balance). The term removes neither the thermal energy of the target electrons nor the ionization energy of a bound target, and DREAM removes neither.

The rate of a FokkerPlanckTerm is its radial transport alone, the divergence of the radial flux Φ = 𝒱'Γʳ of its operator (AdvectionDiffusion), positive outward, through the faces k and k + 1 of the radius k, over the whole grid. P_k [W/m³] is positive where the electrons at radius k gain energy:

\[P_k = -\frac{1}{V'_k Δr_k} \sum_{ij} m_e c^2 (γ_i − 1)\, Δp_i Δξ_j \left(Φ_{ij,k+1} − Φ_{ij,k}\right),\]

with the spatial Jacobian V'_k [m] and the radial width Δr_k [m] of the radius, and the cells of zero volume left out. The fluxes are those of the discretised equation of f: the radial scheme, the overlap of pitch grids that differ between radii and the boundary at the edge. The momentum part of the operator, where it holds one, is not in P. This source takes no region, and a region throws an ArgumentError.

The term reads the coefficients of the operator at every evaluation, so it follows a RechesterRosenbluthUpdate. It is linear in the distribution under a linear radial scheme. Its Jacobian holds the cells that the radial fluxes read: the neighbouring radii, and the second neighbours under a four-point scheme.

The transport only moves energy between the radii and out through the edge: the integral Σ_k V'_k Δr_k P_k is minus the energy flux out through the edge, zero with r_boundary = :zero_flux.

In the row of the temperature under total_energy_equation the system requires this term for each Fokker-Planck term of a distribution of the total energy whose operator holds nonzero radial coefficients. Its source must be that Fokker-Planck term of the system, not a copy, and it takes no region. The system checks the sign of the coefficient, and a coefficient of 1 closes the budget. The moment over the whole grid is a choice of this package (see the docs page Choices of this package): the cold electrons receive the energy that the transport moves with the electrons below the threshold of the hot region, and with the electrons that it carries across that threshold.

The Rechester-Rosenbluth heat diffusivity of HeatTransportTerm is the energy moment of the same kinetic diffusion on a Maxwellian of uniform density, so next to this term it would move the heat of the bulk a second time. The row of the total energy refuses that pair, whatever the level of the heat transport, and accepts a prescribed diffusivity (see the docs page Energy and current balance).

Runes2P.KineticEnergyLossTerm — Type
KineticEnergyLossTerm(source::FokkerPlanckTerm; part=:free_electrons, region=nothing,
                      coefficient=part === :free_electrons ? 1 : -1)

coefficient times the power [W/m³] that the electrons of the kinetic unknown of source lose through a part of their collisions, for the row of the temperature of the cold electrons:

\[L = −\frac{1}{V'} \sum_{ij} w_i\, m_e c^2 (γ_i − 1)\, [C_a f − C_b f]_{ij}\, 𝒱'_{ij} Δp_i Δξ_j,\]

with the envelope w of region (1 without one, MomentumRegion) and the operators of the plasma, collision settings, scheme and lower boundaries of source. Neither part takes a flux through p_max, whatever the upper boundary of source: the electrons that cross there carry their energy across the edge of the grid. That energy is counted by MomentumBoundaryFluxTerm with weight = :energy at an outer boundary and by InterfaceEnergyFluxTerm at the interface to a runaway grid. The parts:

  • part = :free_electrons: C_a the electron-electron part (electron_electron_operator), C_b = 0: the energy that the electrons give to the free cold electrons, the friction and, in the fully kinetic mode, the energy diffusion. For a superthermal electron it is the ∫ v F_s f of DREAM's CollisionalEnergyTransferKineticTerm as the energy change of the operator, without the energy that the friction carries out through p = 0; that term itself, as a moment, is FrictionEnergyTransferTerm. With coefficient = 1 it is the channel of the runaway grid into the total energy of the electrons on the hot-tail grid (total_energy_equation). The row of a total energy refuses this part for a distribution in that total, since the transfer stays inside it.
  • part = :sinks: C_a the collisions of source without the field, C_b their electron-electron part: the energy that leaves the electrons by the friction on bound electrons (excitation and ionization), bremsstrahlung and synchrotron radiation. With coefficient = −1 and the hot region of the total energy as region it is the loss of that total, so the cold electrons receive exactly the free-electron part of what the hot electrons lose, where the parts sum to the momentum flux of source. The parts are the field part, the flux of the electric field alone, the free-electron part and the sinks. They sum to that flux under every momentum scheme but exponential fitting, and under exponential fitting without a field (below). Under exponential fitting with a field they do not, and the rest goes to the cold electrons without a refusal. With a field the cold electrons receive in addition the gap between E j_hot and the work of the discrete field term. Both operators are applied, since the weights of exponential fitting depend on all coefficients of an operator. With bound_friction = :ionization_split of CollisionSettings the friction on bound electrons holds the energy of the kinetic ionization (KineticIonizationTerm) up to the discretization and the width of the threshold (see there): the ionization potentials, which the ions store and release at recombination, and the energy of the secondaries, which SecondaryHeatingTerm gives back to the cold electrons (ionization_energy_flows). With DREAM's :bethe it holds that energy only on average and only where the screening window is open: from the ionization potential up to the mean excitation energy it takes none.

The momentum face values of both operators take the upwind side of the whole advection of source under every momentum scheme with a side: :upwind, :quick, :upwind_second_order and the flux limiters, the four-point schemes among them on a grid of at least three momentum cells. The whole advection is that of the collisions and the electric field, of the plasma or of the field unknown, as the residual of source evaluates them. That face value depends on f and on the side alone, so the field part, the free-electron part and the sinks sum to the momentum flux of source face by face. A flux limiter in ξ or in r takes the same form under any momentum scheme. The term takes the moment in the form of the momentum faces, since the pitch fluxes sum to zero over a momentum row:

\[L = −\frac{1}{V'} \sum_j Δξ_j \sum_k (ε_k − ε_{k−1})\, F_{kj},\]

with ε_k = w_k mₑc²(γ_k − 1) [J] of momentum cell k (ε_0 = 0) and F_{kj} the momentum flux of C_a − C_b through the lower face of cell (k, j) times 𝒱'. The term reads the sign of the whole advection, not of the field alone. It lists the field unknown among its dependencies but has no column of it. Where a face changes its side, each part jumps, while their sum, the momentum flux of source, stays continuous. The Jacobian takes the side as fixed and holds the derivative of the limiter, where there is one.

The momentum schemes without a side, :central and :exponential_fitting, with linear schemes in ξ and r, give the term the moment of the two operators applied to f. The central face value does not depend on the side, so the parts sum to the flux under it as well. The face value of exponential fitting follows the coefficients of each operator, so with a field the parts do not sum to the flux there, with or without a limiter in ξ or r. Without a field the first operator of the sinks holds the coefficients of the source, and the two parts sum to its flux. Where the diffusion of an operator is not positive, as in the superthermal collisions, which have no energy diffusion, exponential fitting takes the upwind side of the advection of that operator alone.

The term takes the plasma of source in the same treatment: implicit, with the columns of the temperature and density unknowns, or lagged at the state of the start of the step. A region whose limit follows the temperature adds the column of its envelope. The term builds its two operators from the plasma at every evaluation of its residual and its Jacobian.

A lagged plasma (lagged = :all of source) is that of the last prepare_step! of the term, which residual! and the right-hand side of the system call before every evaluation, and which the system calls with its initial state for a term never prepared when it builds the prototype of its Jacobian. A term evaluated on its own reads the plasma of its last preparation, by whatever system or call made it, and a term never prepared throws an ArgumentError. Lagged ion densities alone are those of the last prepare_step! of source, or those of the state when source was never prepared.
