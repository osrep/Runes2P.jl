Heating, radiation and heat transport

src/system/radiation_terms.jl

Public

Runes2P.RadiatedPowerTerm — Type
RadiatedPowerTerm(plasma; radiation=:recombination, temperature=:cold_electron_temperature,
                  density=:cold_electron_density, ions=nothing,
                  kinetic_ionization=nothing, extrapolation=:dream,
                  bremsstrahlung_corrections=:gould,
                  directory=adas_cache_directory(), initial_time=0.0)

The power that the cold electrons lose to the ions, −P [W/m³] in the row of their temperature (which holds the time derivative of their energy), at every radius:

\[P = n_e \sum_{i, Z_0} n_{i, Z_0} \left(L_{i, Z_0} + B_{i, Z_0}\right)\]

with the coefficients L and B [W m³] of each charge state, the electron density n_e and temperature from the unknowns density and temperature, and the densities of the charge states n_{i,Z_0} from the ions of plasma, a PlasmaState or a function of time that returns one, or from the radial unknown ions of the charge-state densities of the species of plasma (ion_densities_unknown). The species are named by their element ("Ar", "D"), whose ADF11 tables are read from directory when the term is built (read_adas). A plasma that is a function of time gives these species at the time initial_time [s]. With ions the term assembles its Jacobian with the columns of the charge states (add_local_jacobian!). With kinetic_ionization, the KineticIonizationTerm of the split of IonRateTerm, the binding energy of the ionization is that of the cold electrons below its threshold, at the fluid coefficient of fluid_ionization_coefficient.

  • radiation = :recombination (the default, a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package): the line radiation L = PLT + PRB with the recombination radiation and bremsstrahlung, the binding energy that ionization takes, I(Z_0) SCD, and the one recombination returns, −I(Z_0 − 1) ACD, with the ionization energies I of NIST (ionization_energy). DREAM's mode with recombination takes I(Z_0) in the gain from its second radial cell on.
  • radiation = :dream: DREAM's default, L = PLT, the ionization loss alone, and the bremsstrahlung of Gould with a relativistic correction, C √T (Σ n Z_0² + (a Σ n Z_0² + κ n_e) T/mₑc²) n_e, C = 1.69e-38 W m³ eV^{-1/2} (GOULD_BREMSSTRAHLUNG_PREFACTOR). With bremsstrahlung_corrections = :gould (default) a = 11/24 and κ = 5(44 − 3π²)/(24√2), Gould's values after his erratum and by his own eqs. (18) and (22) (GOULD_ION_CORRECTION, GOULD_ELECTRON_CORRECTION); with :dream DREAM's 19/24 and 5(44 − 3π²)/(8√2). DREAM's radiation therefore needs both radiation = :dream and bremsstrahlung_corrections = :dream; the second has no effect with :recombination. The ion sum is that of the charge states the term reads, n_e the unknown.

The coefficients are those of ADAS (H. P. Summers, The ADAS User Manual, version 2.6 (2004), http://www.adas.ac.uk, with the data of OpenADAS, https://open.adas.ac.uk), DREAM's src/Equations/Fluid/RadiatedPowerTerm.cpp. extrapolation goes to adas_coefficient; its default :dream takes DREAM's extrapolation below the tables (a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package). The derivatives with respect to the temperature and the density are exact through ForwardDiff. radiated_power gives the radiation and the binding energy apart.

Runes2P.ohmic_heating_term — Method
ohmic_heating_term(; electric_field=:electric_field,
                   current_density=:ohmic_current_density)

The ohmic heating j_ohm E [W/m³] of the cold electrons, for the row of their temperature (DREAM's OhmicHeatingTerm, whose geometric weight is 1 in a cylinder): a ProductTerm of the field electric_field [V/m] and the ohmic current density current_density [A/m²]. The heating takes the treatment of the equation that sets the current (the conductivity of a ConductivityTerm), so the two agree by construction.

Runes2P.radiated_power — Method
radiated_power(term::RadiatedPowerTerm, u, layout, t)

The two parts of the power of RadiatedPowerTerm at the state u and the time t [s], per radius [W/m³]: radiation, which leaves the plasma (line radiation, recombination radiation and bremsstrahlung), and binding, the binding energy that the ions take on ionization, net of what recombination returns, which stays in the plasma.

Runes2P.GOULD_BREMSSTRAHLUNG_PREFACTOR — Constant
GOULD_BREMSSTRAHLUNG_PREFACTOR

The prefactor of the bremsstrahlung power of a Maxwellian plasma in the mode :dream of RadiatedPowerTerm, (32/3) α r₀² c √(2 mₑc² e/π) [W m³ eV^-1/2], the 1.69e-38 of the NRL Plasma Formulary: R. J. Gould, "Thermal bremsstrahlung from high-temperature plasmas", Astrophys. J. 238, 1026 (1980), ADS 1980ApJ...238.1026G (erratum Astrophys. J. 243, 677 (1981)), as DREAM's src/Equations/Fluid/RadiatedPowerTerm.cpp (lines 47-52 and 174-189, the 1.69e-38 named at line 179). Gould's expansion holds for 10⁷ to 10⁹ K, about 1 to 100 keV. Neither Runes2P nor DREAM has its first-order Born correction, (ln 2 − 1/2) π (Σ Z³ n/Σ Z² n) (π Ry/kT)^{1/2} (eq. (41) with the erratum's π), which at 10 eV is 1.25 times the leading term for charge 1: at the temperatures of a disruption the formula is outside its range, where the line radiation of the impurities dominates the losses.

Runes2P.GOULD_ELECTRON_CORRECTION — Constant
GOULD_ELECTRON_CORRECTION

The electron-electron bremsstrahlung of a Maxwellian plasma relative to the electron-ion one of charge 1, 5(44 − 3π²)/(24√2) ≈ 2.12 times T/mₑc²: the ratio of eqs. (22) and (18) of R. J. Gould, Astrophys. J. 238, 1026 (1980), the Born approximation of the electron-electron and the electron-ion power, (20/9)(44 − 3π²) π^{-1/2} α³Λ² m^{-1/2} n_e² (kT)^{3/2} over (32/3)(2/π)^{1/2} α³Λ² m c³ n_e Σ Z² n_Z τ^{1/2}; also eq. (58) of H. Xie, "Bremsstrahlung radiation power in fusion plasmas revisited", arXiv:2404.11540 (2024). Gould's summary, eq. (41), which says it combines eqs. (18) and (22), prints 5(44 − 3π²)/(8√2) ≈ 6.36, three times larger, and the erratum (Astrophys. J. 243, 677 (1981)) does not correct it; DREAM takes that value (DREAM_GOULD_ELECTRON_CORRECTION). The factor rests on the consistency of the paper's own equations and on Xie, not on an erratum.

Runes2P.GOULD_ION_CORRECTION — Constant
GOULD_ION_CORRECTION

The first-order relativistic correction 11/24 of the electron-ion bremsstrahlung of a Maxwellian plasma, the coefficient of T/mₑc² in the power of GOULD_BREMSSTRAHLUNG_PREFACTOR: R. J. Gould, Astrophys. J. 238, 1026 (1980), eqs. (18) and (41), as corrected in the erratum, Astrophys. J. 243, 677 (1981) ("In equations (18) and (41), 19/24 should be replaced by 11/24", found by E. Haug). It is the slope in T of the Jüttner average of the cross section 4BN of H. W. Koch and J. W. Motz, Rev. Mod. Phys. 31, 920 (1959), bremsstrahlung_cross_section, which gives 11/24 to 1e-5 (test_independent_references.jl). DREAM's src/Equations/Fluid/RadiatedPowerTerm.cpp:51 keeps the 19/24 of the paper (DREAM_GOULD_ION_CORRECTION).

Runes2P.HeatTransportTerm — Type
HeatTransportTerm(; diffusion=nothing, diffusion_at=nothing, delta_b_over_b=nothing,
                  delta_b_over_b_at=nothing, major_radius=nothing, safety_factor=1.0,
                  temperature=:cold_electron_temperature, density=:cold_electron_density,
                  edge=:zero_flux, coefficient=1)

Radial heat diffusion of the cold electrons, (1/V') ∂_r (V' χ ∂_r T) [W/m³] with T in eV, for the row of their temperature. The flux through the face between two cells is V' χ (T_k − T_{k−1})/Δr with the distance Δr of the two centres, and χ at the face takes the density and the temperature interpolated linearly between the two cells (DREAM's deltaRadialFlux). No heat crosses the first face. One of two models:

  • a diffusivity D [m²/s], χ = 3/2 e D n (DREAM's src/Equations/Fluid/HeatTransportDiffusion.cpp:85-87): diffusion a radial profile (radial_values on the faces), or diffusion_at a function of the time [s] that returns one;
  • the heat diffusivity of Rechester and Rosenbluth in stochastic field lines (A. B. Rechester and M. N. Rosenbluth, Phys. Rev. Lett. 40, 38 (1978), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.40.38; DREAM's src/Equations/Fluid/HeatTransportRechesterRosenbluth.cpp:80 for 3√(2π) c e and :105 for (1 − 5Θ/8), cylinder),

    \[χ = 3\sqrt{2π}\, e\, c\, q R_0 (δB/B)^2\, n \sqrt{Θ} (1 − 5Θ/8),\qquad Θ = T/m_ec^2,\]

    the energy moment of the kinetic diffusion π q R₀ |v_∥| (δB/B)² of add_rechester_rosenbluth_diffusion! on a Maxwellian of uniform density. The part of that moment in ∂_r n is dropped, as in DREAM, whose derivation assumes ∂n_cold/∂r = 0 because the cold electrons are not transported (doc/notes/heat-transport.tex:236-245). delta_b_over_b is a profile, or delta_b_over_b_at a function of the time [s] such as the PiecewiseConstant transport history of a RechesterRosenbluthUpdate. In a row of the cold thermal energy, which the radial transport of a distribution does not enter, the same object in both keeps the kinetic and the heat transport on one level. The major radius R₀ [m] and the safety factor q are inputs, as for the kinetic operator. The default q = 1 is DREAM's, which fixes it (HeatTransportRechesterRosenbluth.cpp:98-100, marked as a TODO there).

In a row of the total energy (total_energy_equation) that holds the energy term of the radial transport of a distribution (KineticEnergyChangeTerm), the system refuses the Rechester-Rosenbluth model, whatever its level: that term already moves the heat of the bulk with its electrons. A prescribed diffusivity is allowed there, as a heat transport of the user's own on top of the kinetic transport. The energy term of the kinetic transport is no heat transport of the temperature: at a uniform temperature it carries heat down a density gradient, and under the Rechester-Rosenbluth diffusion it drives the cold electrons towards a uniform $n T^{3/2}$, not a uniform $T$ (see the docs page Energy and current balance).

A PiecewiseConstant level takes, in the step that ends at a switch time, the value before the switch (level_over_step).

Its switch times must be time points of the run (forced_time_points), so that a step does not straddle a change.

edge is the condition at r = a:

  • :zero_flux (default, DREAM's BC_CONSERVATIVE, the setting of DREAM's disruption examples);
  • :absorbing: T = 0 on the edge face, over the distance from the last centre to the face;
  • :absorbing_dream: DREAM's BC_F_0, T = 0 at the distance of the last two centres;
  • :constant_gradient_dream: DREAM's BC_DF_CONST, the gradient of the last two cells continued one cell outside (two radii at least).

Runes2P.heat_flux_through_edge — Method
heat_flux_through_edge(term::HeatTransportTerm, u, layout, t)

The heat that leaves through the edge of the plasma at the time t [s], −V' χ ∂_r T at r = a [W/m]: zero with edge = :zero_flux. With the spatial Jacobian V' = r of a cylinder, it is the heat per second through the edge per metre of the cylinder divided by 2π. The volume integral Σ V' Δr of the term, in the same unit, is minus this flux.
