Particle sources, boundary fluxes and the avalanche

src/system/population_terms.jl

Public

Runes2P.MaxwellianSourceTerm — Type
MaxwellianSourceTerm(kinetic, grid, temperature; target_density, rate,
                     bulk_momentum=Inf)

Source with the shape of a non-relativistic Maxwellian exp(-p²/2Θ), Θ = T/mₑc², at temperature [eV] (DREAM's ParticleSourceTerm uses the relativistic one), whose magnitude drives the density of the kinetic unknown kinetic back to target_density [m⁻³] (a number, or one value per radius) at the rate rate [1/s]:

S(p, ξ) = rate (n_target - n(f)) f_M(p) / n_M,

with f_M the Maxwellian on grid and n_M its density, so the source adds rate (n_target - n(f)) particles per second and per unit volume and nothing else. kinetic and the row unknown both live on grid. It keeps a thermal bulk from draining into the runaway region, which is what the published Dreicer rates assume; with rate = 1/Δt a step restores the particles the previous step lost.

n(f) counts the cells below bulk_momentum (in units of mₑc), and n_target is the density of the bulk there. The bound matters: with the whole grid counted, particles moving from the bulk into the runaway tail leave the total unchanged, the source sees no deficit, and the bulk still drains. Set it below the critical momentum and above the thermal momentum.

The term is :explicit (evaluated at the previous time level), so the density moment it reads adds nothing to the Jacobian and the sparsity stays local. This is a device for comparisons with the Dreicer rates, not the self-consistent particle source: DREAM's ParticleSourceTerm fixes the magnitude from the cold-electron density through quasi-neutrality (ParticleSourceTerm). The system refuses the term in the equation of a distribution whose energy is in the total energy of the electrons (total_energy_equation), since no term carries the energy of the electrons that it adds or removes.

Runes2P.MomentumBoundaryFluxTerm — Type
MomentumBoundaryFluxTerm(kinetic, source; weight=:density, coefficient=1)

coefficient times the flux of the kinetic unknown kinetic through the upper momentum boundary of its grid, per volume, added to the row of a radial unknown with one component: the electrons that leave the grid at p_max. source holds the coefficients of the flux: the Fokker-Planck term of kinetic (FokkerPlanckTerm or prescribed_fokker_planck_term) or its operator. The flux is that of the boundary condition of the operator at p_max, so the term and the operator count the same particles. With a term that takes the electric field as an unknown, the flux holds the field of the state.

  • weight = :density: the particles per volume and second [m⁻³ s⁻¹], (1/V') Σ_j Δξ_j 𝒱' Γᵖ. With TimeDerivative() the row unknown is the density of the electrons above the grid, or a fluid runaway density fed by the hot-tail grid.
  • weight = :current: the flux weighted with e c ξ, the rate [A m⁻² s⁻¹] of the current density that the electrons carry when they leave, at the speed of light. With TimeDerivative() the row unknown is the current density of the electrons above the grid.
  • weight = :energy: the flux weighted with the kinetic energy mₑc²(γ − 1) of the last momentum cell [W/m³], the energy that the discrete energy moment of the grid loses with the electrons; the energy at the face itself differs by about Δp/(2p). With coefficient = −1 in the row of the temperature it is the energy that leaves the total energy of the electrons on the hot-tail grid (total_energy_equation).

The entries of the row are assembled directly, from the derivative of the flux of each pitch cell with respect to the cells at the boundary. With a Fokker-Planck term whose plasma reads unknowns implicitly (has_plasma_columns) the flux follows them as well: its columns of the temperature, the density and the charge states come from the flux of the term's coefficients in dual numbers, through the weights of the scheme of the boundary (add_plasma_columns!).

Runes2P.ParticleSourceTerm — Type
ParticleSourceTerm(source, grid, temperature; shape=:relativistic_maxwellian)

Particle source of a kinetic unknown on grid with the magnitude of the radial unknown source [1/(m³ s)]: S(r) g(p), with the shape g normalized on the grid to one particle per volume, so that the term adds S particles per volume and second and nothing else (DREAM's ParticleSourceTerm). The magnitude is an unknown whose equation is the constraint it enforces, e.g. 0 = n(f) − n_cold − n_hot with the kind Constraint: the source moves electrons between the cold population and the distribution as electrons run away or the free-electron density changes.

shape:

  • :relativistic_maxwellian: exp(-(γ − 1)/Θ) with Θ = T/mₑc² (DREAM's default);
  • :maxwellian: the non-relativistic exp(-p²/2Θ) of MaxwellianSourceTerm;
  • :lowest_cell: the momentum cell at p = 0 alone (DREAM's delta shape). It does not depend on the temperature, and the stored Jacobian holds the first momentum cell of every pitch cell in the column of the magnitude and no column of the temperature. A temperature that is an unknown stays among the term's dependencies, and the term is not linear (is_linear), although its column of the temperature is zero.

temperature is the cold-electron temperature in eV at each radius, or a function of time that returns the temperatures or a PlasmaState. The shape follows it in time and is rebuilt when the temperature of a radius changes. It is prescribed data: the term is linear, and its Jacobian entries are assembled directly.

temperature may also be the name of the radial unknown of the temperature. The term is then not linear, and its Jacobian holds the column of the temperature, S ∂g/∂T, with the derivative of the normalization on the grid, which DREAM's column leaves out (ParticleSourceTerm.cpp). The momentum integral of that column vanishes, as the source adds S particles at any temperature.

Runes2P.ExternalAvalancheTerm — Type
ExternalAvalancheTerm(plasma; p_cutoff, runaway_density=:runaway_density,
                      total_density=:total_electron_density, electric_field=:electric_field,
                      temperature=:cold_electron_temperature, density=:cold_electron_density,
                      ions=nothing, settings=CollisionSettings(), exponent=-2)

DREAM's ExternalAvalancheTerm (src/Equations/Fluid/ExternalAvalancheTerm.cpp, placed by src/Settings/RunawaySourceTerms.cpp:36-58) in the equation of the fluid runaway density n_re above a hot-tail grid without a runaway grid: dn_re/dt = Γ n_re [m⁻³ s⁻¹] with the rate Γ [s⁻¹]

\[Γ = \left(Γ_\mathrm{ext}^{a} + Γ_\mathrm{fluid}^{a}\right)^{1/a} \text{ for } Γ_\mathrm{fluid} > 0, \qquad Γ = Γ_\mathrm{fluid} \text{ otherwise},\]

a = exponent = −2 (DREAM's value), the Rosenbluth-Putvinski rate Γ_ext [s⁻¹] of the knock-ons above p_cutoff (units of mₑc), Γ_ext = n_tot rosenbluth_putvinski_rate(p_cutoff), and DREAM's fluid growth rate [s⁻¹] with the effective critical field, fluid_avalanche_growth_rate. The match makes the external rate, positive at every field, follow the fluid rate below the effective critical field, where it decays. DREAM takes p_cutoff as the upper momentum face of the hot-tail grid, p_max (RunawaySourceTerms.cpp:44-46), not the cutoff of the kinetic source (RosenbluthPutvinskiSourceTerm), and so must the case. One p_cutoff serves every radius, as in DREAM ("assume same momentum grid at all radii", RunawaySourceTerms.cpp:41): a grid whose p_max differs between radii needs a term per radius.

The plasma of the rate is that of plasma (a PlasmaState or a function of time: the magnetic field, the composition) with the temperature temperature, the cold density density and, with ions, the charge states of that radial unknown, as the other fluid terms read them; settings gives the screening, the Coulomb logarithm and the tables of DREAM's RunawayFluid (runaway_fluid_settings). The Jacobian is exact: the columns of n_re, n_tot, the field, the temperature, the density and the charge states by forward differentiation through the solutions of the effective critical field and of p⋆ (one Newton correction in dual numbers each). DREAM's own Jacobian takes the columns of the field and of n_tot alone, with the approximation Γ ∝ |E| − E_ceff (RunawayFluid.cpp:881-907). The solution of the effective critical field takes about 2.4 ms per radius on one core (the D and Ar of DREAM's disruption example; 2.5 ms with the derivatives). The rate of a radius is evaluated once per state: the rows that hold the term (the runaway density and the particle source of a hot-tail grid with fluid runaways) and the residual and the Jacobian at one state share the evaluation, and the Jacobian takes every partial in one pass of forward differentiation.

Runes2P.RosenbluthPutvinskiSourceTerm — Type
RosenbluthPutvinskiSourceTerm(kinetic, grid; runaway_density=:runaway_density,
                              total_density=:total_electron_density,
                              electric_field=:electric_field, p_cutoff=0.01)

DREAM's kinetic Rosenbluth-Putvinski avalanche source (AVALANCHE_MODE_KINETIC, src/Equations/Kinetic/AvalancheSourceRP.cpp, placed by src/Settings/Equations/f_hot.cpp:116-127) in the equation of the kinetic unknown kinetic on grid: the knock-on electrons that a fluid runaway density n_re above the grid creates on the electrons of the total density n_tot (free and bound), at every momentum cell above p_cutoff (units of mₑc) and at the pitch ξ* = ±√((γ − 1)/(γ + 1)) that the momentum of a knock-on from a primary at the speed of light fixes, in the cell that holds it:

\[S_{ij} = n_\mathrm{re} n_\mathrm{tot}\, r_0^2 c\, \frac{1}{Δp_i}\left(\frac{1}{γ_{i−1/2}^* − 1} − \frac{1}{γ_{i+1/2} − 1}\right) \frac{2π V'}{\mathcal{V}'_{ij} Δξ_j},\]

with γ*_{i−1/2} at the larger of the lower face and p_cutoff and Δp_i the whole cell. The source S_ij [m⁻³ s⁻¹] is the rate of the distribution [m⁻³] in the cell (i, j). Summed over the grid it is rosenbluth_putvinski_rate(p_cutoff, p_max) times n_re n_tot exactly. The pitch is that of DREAM in a cylinder (B_max = B_min, fvm/Grid/FluxSurfaceAverager.avalancheDelta.cpp:114-140): ξ* takes the sign of the electric field of each radius (RP_SOURCE_PITCH_ADAPTIVE), or + without electric_field, and a ξ* on a cell face gives no cell (DREAM's strict inequalities). The term is bilinear in n_re and n_tot; the sign of the field enters without a derivative, as in DREAM. The Jacobian is assembled directly.

The source creates electrons, which the particle source of the grid takes out of the cold electrons: DREAM's row of the particle source, S = dn_free/dt − internal − external, holds the internal number with the factor −1 (TotalElectronDensityFromKineticAvalanche, f_hot.cpp:244-255, 362-374). Written as dn_free/dt = S + internal + external (TimeDerivative(:free_electron_density)), the term is ProductTerm(total_density, runaway_density; coefficient = +rosenbluth_putvinski_rate(p_cutoff, p_max)). The value p_cutoff = 0.01 is that of DREAM's disruption example (examples/Disruptions/generate.py:329).

Runes2P.rosenbluth_putvinski_rate — Function
rosenbluth_putvinski_rate(p_lower, p_upper=Inf)

The rate of the Rosenbluth-Putvinski avalanche source between the momenta p_lower and p_upper (units of mₑc) per runaway and per electron of the total density, in m³/s:

\[\frac{1}{n_\mathrm{re} n_\mathrm{tot}} \int_{p_\mathrm{lower}}^{p_\mathrm{upper}} S\,\mathrm{d}^3p = 2π r_0^2 c \left(\frac{1}{γ_\mathrm{lower} − 1} − \frac{1}{γ_\mathrm{upper} − 1}\right),\]

the knock-ons of a runaway at the speed of light on electrons at rest: the Møller cross section in the limit of an infinitely fast primary, 2π r₀²/(γ − 1)² per unit γ (M. N. Rosenbluth and S. V. Putvinski, Nucl. Fusion 37, 1355 (1997), doi:10.1088/0029-5515/37/10/I03, eq. (4)). DREAM's AvalancheSourceRP::EvaluateNormalizedTotalKnockOnNumber (src/Equations/Kinetic/AvalancheSourceRP.cpp:96-115), with its prefactor e⁴/((4πε₀ mₑ c)² c) = r₀² c. Infinite at p_lower = 0.

Runes2P.AvalancheRateCache — Type
AvalancheRateCache

The last evaluation of the matched rate of ExternalAvalancheTerm at one radius, shared by the rows that hold the term and by the residual and the Jacobian at one state: the local values x and the time t of the entry, the value of the rate times n_re, and its derivatives by x once the Jacobian needed them. One entry per radius, so that a loop over radii in threads writes separate entries. The entries take a state in Float64; a state in another number type is evaluated without the cache.
