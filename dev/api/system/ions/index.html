MAXIMUM_EQUILIBRIUM_ITERATIONS

Runes2P.IonDiffusionTerm — Type
IonDiffusionTerm(composition; diffusion=nothing, diffusion_at=nothing, ions=:ion_densities,
                 states=:charged, edge=:zero_flux)

Radial diffusion of the densities of the ion charge states of composition, (1/V') ∂_r (V' D ∂_r n_j) [m⁻³ s⁻¹] in the rows of the ion densities ions (DREAM's IonChargedPrescribedDiffusionTerm). The diffusivity D [m²/s] is diffusion, a radial profile on the faces (radial_values), or diffusion_at, a function of the time [s] that returns one, such as a PiecewiseConstant transport history: its switch times must be time points of the run (forced_time_points). The flux through the face between two cells is V' D (n_k − n_{k−1})/Δr with the distance of the centres, as for the heat (HeatTransportTerm); nothing crosses the first face.

  • states = :charged (default) diffuses the charge states Z0 ≥ 1, DREAM's charged diffusion; :all the neutrals too.
  • edge = :zero_flux keeps the ions in the plasma; :absorbing sets the density to zero on the edge face, over the distance from the last centre to the face.

The term has the sign convention of every other row: a positive rate adds particles (DREAM's ion diffusion needs a negative coefficient for the sign of its ion transient term, src/Equations/Fluid/IonTransientTerm.cpp:78).

Runes2P.IonRateTerm — Type
IonRateTerm(composition; ions=:ion_densities, temperature=:cold_electron_temperature,
            density=:cold_electron_density, kinetic_ionization=nothing,
            extrapolation=:dream, directory=adas_cache_directory())

The ionization and recombination of the charge states of composition (IonComposition) by the cold electrons, in the rows of the ion densities ions (DREAM's IonRateEquation):

\[\frac{dn_j}{dt} = n_e \left(I_{j-1} n_{j-1} - I_j n_j + R_{j+1} n_{j+1} - R_j n_j\right)\]

for each species and charge state j = 0, …, Z, with the ionization coefficients I (ADF11 SCD) and the recombination coefficients R (ACD), both in m³/s, of OpenADAS (H. P. Summers, The ADAS User Manual, version 2.6 (2004), http://www.adas.ac.uk, with the data of OpenADAS, https://open.adas.ac.uk) at the electron density n_e and temperature of the radial unknowns density and temperature (adas_coefficient with extrapolation), as DREAM's src/Equations/Fluid/IonRateEquation.cpp. The default extrapolation = :dream takes DREAM's extrapolation below the tables (a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package). The species are named by their element, whose tables are read from directory when the term is built (read_adas).

The sum over the charge states of a species is zero, so the term moves particles between the states and conserves each species. Its Jacobian comes from the colored AD of its residual, with the derivatives of the coefficients through ForwardDiff in place of DREAM's one-sided differences, and without DREAM's floors on the density (1 m⁻³) and the coefficients (1e-40) in the columns of the temperature and the density.

kinetic_ionization, a KineticIonizationTerm of the same system, splits the ionization at the threshold of its region: the electrons of f above the threshold ionize through the cross sections of that term, and the cold electrons below it through the fluid coefficient of fluid_ionization_coefficient, the ADAS rate less the part of the Maxwellian above the threshold. No electron is counted twice. The threshold is a multiple of the thermal momentum of at least the factor of MINIMUM_SPLIT_THRESHOLD_FACTORS for the form of its cross sections. Without kinetic_ionization the fluid rate is the whole ADAS rate, which next to a kinetic ionization term is DREAM's choice (ions.cpp:280): the Maxwellian tail above the threshold ionizes twice.

Runes2P.WeightedSumTerm — Type
WeightedSumTerm(unknown, weights)

Σ_k w_k x_k over the components x_k of the radial unknown unknown at each radius, added to the row of a radial unknown with one component: the free-electron density n_free = Σ Z0 n_ij of the ion densities with the weights of free_electron_weights. The weights take the unit of the components to the unit of the row. The term is linear, and its Jacobian entries are assembled directly, since its row holds every component of a radius.

Runes2P.coronal_equilibrium — Method
coronal_equilibrium(term::IonRateTerm, s, free_electron_density, temperature;
                    species_density=1)

The densities of the charge states of species s in coronal equilibrium at the free-electron density free_electron_density [m⁻³] and the temperature temperature [eV], with the total density of the species species_density, whose unit the densities take (m⁻³ for a density, the fractions of the species for the default 1): the steady state of the rates of IonRateTerm, where the flow between neighbouring states vanishes, n_{j+1}/n_j = I_j/R_{j+1}. The ratios are multiplied in log space, so states far from the dominant one underflow to 0 and the large ratios at the extremes of the temperature do not overflow (DREAM multiplies the ratios directly, src/Settings/IonEquilibrium.cpp:100-137).

Runes2P.deposit_neutrals! — Method
deposit_neutrals!(u, layout, composition, deposits...; ions=:ion_densities)

Add neutral atoms to the unknown ions of the state u at the start of a run (DREAM's IONS_DYNAMIC_NEUTRAL): each deposit species => density adds density [m⁻³], a number or one value per radius, to the neutral state of the species of that name. The rate equations ionize them from the first step on. DREAM's reference case deposits neutral deuterium and argon at 1 eV, uniform in radius (examples/Disruptions/generate.py); a deposit of deuterium into the species of the background deuterium gives the same densities as DREAM's separate species, since the rates are linear in the densities.

Runes2P.fill_coronal_equilibrium! — Method
fill_coronal_equilibrium!(u, layout, term::IonRateTerm, temperature;
                          species=term.composition.names)
fill_coronal_equilibrium!(u, layout, composition, temperature; species=composition.names,
                          ions=:ion_densities, extrapolation=:dream,
                          directory=adas_cache_directory())

Put the species species (a name, as a String or a Symbol, or a collection of names) of the composition of term, an IonRateTerm, in coronal equilibrium in the state u, in the radial unknown of the ion densities of term, at the temperature temperature [eV], a profile in any form of radial_values on the cell centres. The total density of each of these species is that of u and is kept; the other species keep their charge states. The rates are those of term with its extrapolation; its kinetic_ionization is not used, as DREAM's init_equil takes the fluid rates alone. The second method builds the term from composition (IonComposition), ions, extrapolation and directory, which reads the ADF11 tables of every species. Returns u.

The equilibrium depends on the free-electron density, which it changes, so the free-electron density is iterated to a fixed point at each radius as DREAM's init_equil does (EvaluateIonEquilibrium, src/Settings/IonEquilibrium.cpp:63-155, DREAM v26.5): it starts at the free electrons of the other species plus the total density of each species in equilibrium, the charge states of coronal_equilibrium follow at that density, and the free-electron density is summed from them, until it changes by at most eps() of itself or after MAXIMUM_EQUILIBRIUM_ITERATIONS iterations. A radius where that density starts at or below zero, where the species in equilibrium have no atoms and the other species no free electrons, is left as it is: no rates are defined there.

The free-electron and cold-electron densities of the state are not changed: the caller sets them from the new charge states, as the rows of the system give them (population_equations, fill_initial_values!) or by hand.

Throws an ArgumentError for a species that is not in the composition or is named twice, for a radial unknown of the ion densities whose components are not those of the composition, for a total density of a species in equilibrium that is negative or not finite, or for a temperature that is not positive or not finite, and a DimensionMismatch for a profile with another number of values than radii; these leave u as it was.

Runes2P.IonEnergyExchangeTerm — Type
IonEnergyExchangeTerm(plasma; side, ion_energy=:ion_thermal_energy,
                      temperature=:cold_electron_temperature, density=:cold_electron_density,
                      ion_ion=true, ion_coulomb_logarithm=:nrl, masses=nothing, mass_table=:standard,
                      ions=nothing, initial_time=0.0)

The collisional exchange of thermal energy between Maxwellian species (DREAM's src/Equations/Fluid/MaxwellianCollisionalEnergyTransferTerm.cpp, the prefactor at line 49), in W/m³: the power that species a receives from species b,

\[Q_{ab} = \sqrt{3/π}\,\frac{(e^2/ε_0)^2 \sqrt{m_a m_b}}{4π} \ln Λ\, \sqrt{N_a N_b}\, (nZ^2)_a (nZ^2)_b\, \frac{N_a W_b − N_b W_a}{(m_b N_b W_a + m_a N_a W_b)^{3/2}},\]

the two-temperature relaxation of Spitzer (L. Spitzer, Physics of Fully Ionized Gases, 2nd ed., Interscience, 1962), Q_{ab} = (3/2) N_a ν_ε (T_b − T_a) with the energy relaxation rate of the NRL Plasma Formulary (A. S. Richardson, NRL/PU/6770–19-652, Naval Research Laboratory, 2019). For the electrons N = (nZ²) = n_e and W = 3/2 e n_e T_e from the unknowns density and temperature; for an ion species N is its total density, (nZ²) = Σ n_{Z₀} Z₀² (neutrals hold energy and exchange none), and W the component of the unknown ion_energy (J/m³, one component per species of plasma, in its order). The logarithm of electrons and ions is the thermal one, as DREAM's. Among the ions it is

  • ion_coulomb_logarithm = :nrl (default): the mixed ion-ion logarithm of the NRL Plasma Formulary (A. S. Richardson, NRL/PU/6770–19-652, Naval Research Laboratory, 2019, p. 34), λ_ab = 23 − ln[Z_a Z_b (μ_a + μ_b)/(μ_a T_b + μ_b T_a) (Σ_γ n_γ Z_γ²/T_γ)^{1/2}] with the densities in cm⁻³, the temperatures in eV and μ = m/m_p. Formula (c) of the formulary sums over the two colliding ions; its definition of the screening length on the same page sums over every species whose thermal speed exceeds the relative speed of the pair, ū² < kT_γ/m_γ, which holds for the electrons unless the ions are hotter by the mass ratio, so the sum here takes every ion species and the electrons (n_e/T_e). Each ion species takes its own temperature T = W/(3/2 e N) and the electrons theirs, at least the 0.5 eV of DEFAULT_TEMPERATURE_FLOOR, and for Z its mean charge (nZ²)/(nZ), (nZ) = Σ n_{Z₀} Z₀. The formula holds for λ ≫ 1 (its corrections are O(1/λ)). With Ti = Te, deuterium at 10²⁰ m⁻³, argon at 10¹⁹ m⁻³ in the charge state that dominates in collisional ionization equilibrium in the limit of zero density (P. Bryans, E. Landi and D. W. Savin, Astrophys. J. 691, 1540 (2009), doi:10.1088/0004-637X/691/2/1540, Table 2) and quasi-neutral electrons, it is 8.7 for D with Ar³⁺ at 10 eV and 9.7 for D with Ar⁸⁺ at 54 eV. At the density of this plasma the state of 10 eV lies about one charge state higher: coronal_equilibrium with the ADAS rates at ne = 1.3 × 10²⁰ m⁻³ has Ar⁴⁺ dominant, and D with Ar⁴⁺ gives 8.3 (ne = 1.4 × 10²⁰ m⁻³); at 54 eV it keeps Ar⁸⁺. Ar¹⁶⁺ left at 10 eV by a fast cooling of the electrons, out of equilibrium, gives 5.9 with D (ne = 2.6 × 10²⁰ m⁻³). Ar¹⁶⁺ with Ar¹⁶⁺ gives 3.1 when the argon is held in two species, together at 10¹⁹ m⁻³: a species is not paired with itself, so a plasma of one argon species has the pair D–Ar alone. The formula falls below zero for two species of a highly charged ion, cold and dense: at these densities and with Ti = T_e, Ar¹⁶⁺ with Ar¹⁶⁺ crosses zero at 1.26 eV and gives −1.4 at the floor of 0.5 eV, where D with Ar¹⁶⁺ still gives 1.4. It is cut at 0 so that the exchange keeps its sign, a guard against cold iterates of the solver and cold prescribed input.

  • ion_coulomb_logarithm = :dream: DREAM's 17.3 − ½ ln(n_free/10²⁰) + 3/2 ln(T_e/1 keV) with the electron temperature and the free-electron density, the same for every pair (src/Equations/CoulombLogarithm.cpp:107-113, with no source named there; BootstrapCurrent.cpp:181 calls the form Wesson's, which takes the ion temperature). With Te in place of Ti it is too small where the ions are hotter than the electrons: 10.4 at Te = 10 eV and 10²⁰ m⁻³ against 14.9 of the NRL form for two deuterium species at Ti = 1 keV (the exchange among them 1.4 times too slow after the thermal quench). Without the charges it is too large for a pair with a multiply charged ion: 10.3 at Ti = Te = 10 eV and n_free = 1.3 × 10²⁰ m⁻³ against 8.7 of the NRL form for deuterium with Ar³⁺ in the plasma above (the exchange about 1.2 times too fast).

  • side = :ions, in the row of ion_energy: each component receives Q_{i,e} and, with ion_ion, Σ_j Q_{ij}.

  • side = :electrons, in the row of the temperature (which holds the time derivative of the electron energy): −Σ_i Q_{i,e}.

A system needs both sides on the same unknowns, so the exchange conserves energy; a system with one side alone is refused. The masses are those of ion_mass with mass_table (:standard, the standard atomic weights and the nuclei of the hydrogen isotopes, or :dream, DREAM's rule). masses, when given, replaces them for all species: one mass in kg per species of plasma, in its order. A mass outside 1e-27 kg to 1e-24 kg is refused, which catches a mass given in atomic mass units or in grams. The species are read from plasma at the time initial_time [s]. The densities of the ions come from the prescribed plasma, a PlasmaState or a function of time, or with ions from that radial unknown of the charge states, whose columns the term then assembles directly; a species of zero density is skipped. ion_ion = true adds the exchange among the ion species, as DREAM does whenever the ion energies are evolved (src/Settings/Equations/T_i.cpp:109). A species of zero total density exchanges nothing and gets no derivative there: the exchange goes as the square root of the density, whose derivative at zero is singular. A Newton iterate with a negative ion energy makes the denominator non-positive, where the exchange is zero with a zero derivative: declare the unknown non-negative, RadialUnknown(:ion_thermal_energy; components, nonnegative = true).

Runes2P.KineticIonizationTerm — Type
KineticIonizationTerm(composition, kinetic, grid; ions=:ion_densities, region=nothing,
                      ionization_cross_section_form=:chianti)

The ionization of the charge states of composition (IonComposition) by the electrons of the kinetic unknown kinetic on grid, in the rows of the ion densities ions (DREAM's IonKineticIonizationTerm):

\[\frac{dn_j}{dt} = K_{j-1} n_{j-1} - K_j n_j, \qquad K_j = \frac{1}{V'} \sum_{\text{cells}} e(p)\, v σ_j(p)\, 𝒱' Δp Δξ\, f,\]

with the ionization rate K_j [s⁻¹] of the charge state j, the cross sections ionization_cross_section of the form ionization_cross_section_form (:chianti, the default, CHIANTI's parameters continued relativistically, with DREAM's form for the elements CHIANTI's table lacks; :dream, DREAM's form and fits; or :bethe, DREAM's form with its relativistic term divided by β², refitted), tabulated at the cell centres of the momentum grid of each radius (DREAM tabulates the grid of radius 0 alone), and the envelope e(p) of the MomentumRegion region: the hot electrons above a threshold that may follow the temperature unknown, or the whole grid without a region. Each species is conserved.

Paired with an IonRateTerm through its kinetic_ionization keyword, the electrons below the threshold ionize at the fluid coefficient of fluid_ionization_coefficient, so a Maxwellian f ionizes at the ADAS rate for every threshold. The term has no energy of its own: the friction on bound electrons of the Fokker-Planck term already takes energy from f for the inelastic collisions. With bound_friction = :ionization_split of CollisionSettings and its ionization_threshold at the factor of this term's region, that friction holds the energy I_j + ⟨E_s⟩_j of each ionization the term counts, up to the discretization. Without a region the two agree to the discretization; at a threshold the friction takes a smooth step of its own on the faces (ionization_threshold_width) while this term takes the envelope of its region in the cells, so the two differ by the electrons within that width of the threshold.

The Jacobian is assembled directly: the columns of f (every cell of a radius, the block DREAM's reference case drops), of the charge states, and of the temperature through the threshold of the region.

Runes2P.SecondaryHeatingTerm — Type
SecondaryHeatingTerm(kinetic_ionization::KineticIonizationTerm;
                     widths=SECONDARY_SPECTRUM_WIDTHS,
                     temperature=:cold_electron_temperature, cut=:none, spectrum=:beb,
                     knockon_term=nothing)

The heating [W/m³] of the cold electrons by the secondary electrons of the kinetic ionization kinetic_ionization, for the row of their temperature:

\[Q = e \sum_{s,j} n_{s,j} \frac{1}{V'} \sum_{\text{cells}} e(p)\, v σ_j(p)\, ⟨E_s⟩_j(E)\, 𝒱' Δp Δξ\, f,\]

with the rates of kinetic_ionization, the envelope e(p) of its region (1 without one) and the energy ⟨E_s⟩_j [eV] per ionization of the secondaries of the spectrum spectrum and the cut cut below. A kinetically ionized electron joins the cold electrons through the closure of the cold density; without this term it is born at zero energy.

  • cut = :none (the default): the mean over every secondary, up to (E − I)/2. All secondaries join the cold electrons, also those born above the hot threshold, so this is the moment of the spectrum over the secondaries that the cold electrons receive, and the one that the friction with bound_friction = :ionization_split takes from f (CollisionSettings). Next to a KnockOnTerm on the bound electrons, which also puts the secondaries above its cutoff into a distribution, it counts their energy twice.
  • cut = :threshold: the mean below the hot threshold E_c, conditional on lying there, for every secondary.
  • cut = :knockon: the energy per ionization of the secondaries below the cutoff momentum of the knock-on term knockon_term at each radius (secondary_energy_below, beb_secondary_energy_below). The secondaries above it count with zero energy. A distribution receives them only where the target density of knockon_term counts the bound electrons and its knock-on grid holds their momenta, since the knock-on term produces only into the cells of that grid between the cutoff and the kinematic maximum (KnockOnKernels). The term reads the cutoff of knockon_term at each evaluation, so it follows KnockOnCutoffUpdate, and the cutoff, which is no unknown, adds no column to the Jacobian. The knock-on term creates its secondaries with the cross section of Møller on its own target density, so the two terms give the energy of the spectrum spectrum only as far as the two cross sections agree above the cutoff.

EquationSystem requires knockon_term to be a term of the system whose primary is the kinetic unknown of kinetic_ionization. Next to a knock-on term of that primary on the unknown :total_electron_density, which counts the bound electrons, it refuses cut = :none and cut = :threshold, and knockon_term must be such a term. It refuses cut = :knockon with a knock-on term on :cold_electron_density or :runaway_density, which count free electrons only. It does not check a target density given as values or under another name. In a row of the total energy (total_energy_equation) it refuses the term when the region of kinetic_ionization differs from the region of the energy moment of its distribution: the heating would then give the cold electrons the energy of ionizations by electrons outside the hot region, which no term charges.

The secondaries above the cutoff carry a large share of the energy. As an illustration, take the secondaries above 1 keV of the ionization of a neutral atom by a primary of 1.3 MeV, two energies chosen only for the example:

  • With the binary-encounter-Bethe spectrum, the default, they carry 41.8 % of the energy of every secondary for Ar⁰ and 41.1 % for D⁰.
  • With the spectrum of Opal et al., they carry 58.4 % for Ar⁰ (width 10.0 eV) and 57.5 % for D⁰ (width 8.3 eV).

spectrum = :beb (the default, a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package) takes the binary-encounter-Bethe spectrum of Kim and Rudd with the ionization energy of each charge state as its binding energy (beb_mean_secondary_energy), which needs no width; spectrum = :opal the spectrum of Opal et al. with the widths widths [eV], the neutral element's for every charge state. The friction of the Fokker-Planck term on bound electrons takes the energy of the ionization from f already, so in the total energy of the electrons the term moves the energy of the secondaries from that sink to the cold electrons. That holds where the friction takes the energy. With the default screening window of the collisions (screening_window = :excitation_energy) the friction on bound electrons is zero from the ionization potential up to the mean excitation energy of the state (about 190 eV for Ar⁰, more for the higher states): there f ionizes without paying, and this term adds the energy of the secondaries on top. screening_window = :dream narrows that band, and bound_friction = :ionization_split of CollisionSettings closes it: f then pays the ionization potential and the energy of the secondaries for every ionization above the threshold, and this term returns the energy of the secondaries to the cold electrons. DREAM has no such term: kinetic_ionization alone is DREAM's model. The region of kinetic_ionization sets E_c; its threshold follows the temperature unknown temperature or is fixed.

Runes2P.fluid_ionization_coefficient — Method
fluid_ionization_coefficient(adas, fraction, coefficient)

The ionization coefficient [m³/s] of the cold electrons below the threshold of a kinetic ionization: (I − A_>)/(1 − f_>), the ADAS coefficient I (adas, in m³/s) of the whole Maxwellian less its part above the threshold, A_> (coefficient, in m³/s), per electron below it, with the fraction f_> of the Maxwellian above the threshold (threshold_split). With the closure n_cold = n_M (1 − f_>) and the kinetic rate n_M A_> of a Maxwellian f of density n_M, the two add up to n_M I for every threshold the grid resolves.

The coefficient has no floor on its value. Its denominator is bounded by the grid: threshold_split takes 1 − f_> as the weight of the first momentum cell where the threshold lies in that cell, and the sum then holds only to within that part. A_> comes from the cross sections of the kinetic ionization and I from ADAS. The Maxwellian rate coefficient of DREAM's fits exceeds the ADAS coefficient by up to a factor of about 1.6 for a state of argon, that of CHIANTI's cross sections by up to 2.6 for a state of neon or argon (Ne²⁺ at 3 to 20 eV). Where the threshold lies below the ionization energy of a state, almost all of its Maxwellian ionization comes from above the threshold, so A_> > I and the coefficient is negative. On the grid of the tests (n_e = 5e19 m⁻³, 5 to 50 eV), with DREAM's cross sections, it is negative for the dominant argon states at threshold factors 2 and 3, by 3 to 18 % of I, and for no state with a coronal fraction above 1e-17 at factors 5 and 7. From 1 eV to 10 keV, for D, Ne and Ar, it is negative for no state with a coronal fraction above 1e-3 with DREAM's cross sections at factors 5 to 7. With CHIANTI's it is negative at factor 5 for three such states of a cold plasma: Ar⁺ at 1 eV (−0.19 I, coronal fraction 0.087), Ar²⁺ at 1.6 eV (−0.50 I, 1.2e-3) and Ne²⁺ at 2.5 eV (−2.8 I, 0.025), and for none at factors 6 and 7. With a Maxwellian f the sum with the kinetic rate is n_M I whatever the sign. The split refuses thresholds below the factor of MINIMUM_SPLIT_THRESHOLD_FACTORS of its form.

Runes2P.ionization_energy_flows — Method
ionization_energy_flows(kinetic_ionization::KineticIonizationTerm, u, layout, t;
                        widths=SECONDARY_SPECTRUM_WIDTHS, spectrum=:beb)

The energy [W/m³ per radius] that the kinetic ionization kinetic_ionization takes from f at the state u and the time t [s], in two parts:

  • potential: the ionization potentials, e Σ I_j K_j n_j, stored in the ions and released at recombination (the gain of RadiatedPowerTerm(...; radiation = :recombination));
  • secondaries: the kinetic energy of every secondary electron, the heating of SecondaryHeatingTerm with cut = :none, whatever cut the system takes; with spectrum = :opal and the widths widths [eV], nothing when an element of the composition has no width of the secondary spectrum (SECONDARY_SPECTRUM_WIDTHS), since potential needs none.

Both are part of the sink of f through the friction on bound electrons (KineticEnergyLossTerm with part = :sinks), so an energy diagnostic that books that sink as lost and the recombination radiation as radiated counts potential twice: it is the share of the sink that goes to the potential energy of the ions.

Runes2P.threshold_split — Method
threshold_split(term::KineticIonizationTerm, ir, u, layout, t)

The parts of a Maxwellian at the cold temperature of radius ir above the threshold of the region of term, on its momentum grid, for fluid_ionization_coefficient: the fraction f_> of its density and the rate coefficient A_> [m³/s] of each charge state, Σ e(p) v σ(p) M w / Σ M w with the envelope e, the cell weights w of a density moment and the relativistic Maxwellian M = exp(−(γ − 1)/Θ) at the cell centres. The sums run over the cells of the grid, the quadrature of the term itself. The temperature is that of the region's unknown in the state u, in its number type.

A threshold p₀ in the first momentum cell, below its upper face, is not resolved by the grid: the part of the Maxwellian below it then comes from the envelope within one cell, not from the shape of the Maxwellian, and tends to 0 as the threshold nears the lower face, while the fluid coefficient divides by it. There 1 − f_> is taken as the weight of that cell, M_1 w_1 / Σ M w (a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package): at the threshold factors the split takes, 5 thermal momenta or more, a threshold in the first cell leaves nearly the whole Maxwellian in that cell, below the threshold. A warning (once per session) names the radius and the temperature; a finer first momentum cell, or a threshold of more thermal momenta, resolves it.
