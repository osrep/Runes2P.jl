Physical constants

Runes2P.Constants

Runes2P.Constants — Module

Physical constants in SI units (electron rest energy in eV), with the values used by DREAM (src/Constants.cpp) so that coefficients can be compared with DREAM to round-off.

source
Runes2P.Constants.BOHR_RADIUS — Constant
BOHR_RADIUS

The Bohr radius [m], 5.29177210903e-11, of CODATA 2018 (E. Tiesinga et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 93, 025010 (2021), doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.93.025010), DREAM's Constants::a0 (src/Constants.cpp).

source
Runes2P.Constants.PROTON_MASS — Constant
PROTON_MASS

The proton mass [kg], 1.67262192369e-27, of CODATA 2018 (E. Tiesinga et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 93, 025010 (2021), doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.93.025010), DREAM's Constants::mH (src/Constants.cpp): the unit of the ion masses of the NRL Plasma Formulary.

source
Runes2P.Constants.RYDBERG_ENERGY — Constant
RYDBERG_ENERGY

The Rydberg energy [eV], 13.605693123, of CODATA 2018 (E. Tiesinga et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 93, 025010 (2021), doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.93.025010) to the digits of DREAM's Constants::Ry (src/Constants.cpp).
