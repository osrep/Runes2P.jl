Runes2P.coulomb_logarithm — Method
coulomb_logarithm(collision, model, p, temperature, free_electron_density)

Coulomb logarithm for :electron_electron or :electron_ion collisions at momentum p (units of mₑc), the temperature temperature [eV] and the free-electron density free_electron_density [m⁻³]. model is :energy_dependent, :thermal or :relativistic (the constant relativistic value, DREAM's CONSTANT).

Runes2P.relativistic_coulomb_logarithm — Method
relativistic_coulomb_logarithm(temperature, free_electron_density)

Relativistic Coulomb logarithm 14.6 + ½ ln(T/(n/10²⁰)) (DREAM lnLambda_c) for the temperature temperature (T) [eV] and the free-electron density free_electron_density (n) [m⁻³], zero for a vanishing density.

source
Runes2P.thermal_coulomb_logarithm — Method
thermal_coulomb_logarithm(temperature, free_electron_density)

Thermal Coulomb logarithm 14.9 - ½ ln(n/10²⁰) + ln(T/keV) for the temperature temperature (T) [eV] and the free-electron density free_electron_density (n) [m⁻³], zero for a vanishing density.

Runes2P.CollisionModel — Type
CollisionModel(plasma, settings)

Collision frequencies for a background plasma. Radius-dependent quantities (free and bound electron densities, Bessel functions, atomic parameters of the charge states) are evaluated once; the frequencies are then evaluated at any momentum with slowing_down_frequency, deflection_frequency and parallel_diffusion_frequency. The Coulomb logarithm is evaluated in each call, since the energy-dependent model depends on the momentum.

Runes2P.CollisionSettings — Type
CollisionSettings(; mode, screening, coulomb_logarithm, screened_diffusion, ion_size_table,
                  screening_window, screening_window_start, screening_window_end, bremsstrahlung,
                  bremsstrahlung_diffusion, bremsstrahlung_constant, bound_friction, ionization_threshold,
                  ionization_threshold_width, secondary_widths, secondary_spectrum, ionization_cross_section_form)

The defaults of mode, screening, coulomb_logarithm, screened_diffusion and bound_friction are those of DREAM's CollisionHandler. The defaults of ion_size_table, screening_window, bremsstrahlung, bremsstrahlung_diffusion and bremsstrahlung_constant depart from DREAM (see the docs page Comparison with DREAM). The remaining options have no counterpart in DREAM.

  • mode: :full (relativistic Maxwellian targets, adds energy diffusion), :superthermal (cold target electrons) or :ultra_relativistic.

  • screening: :partial (Hesslow et al. 2018), :complete or :none (DREAM's PARTIALLY_SCREENED, COMPLETELY_SCREENED, NON_SCREENED).

  • coulomb_logarithm: :thermal, :energy_dependent or :relativistic (see coulomb_logarithm).

  • screened_diffusion: energy diffusion from the full ν_s (:maxwellian) or from the free-electron part only (:zero).

  • ion_size_table: :hesslow uses Table 1 of Hesslow et al. 2018 as intended; :dream reproduces DREAM's lookup, whose charge list has one argon entry too few (affects Ar¹⁷⁺, Xe³⁺, Xe¹⁷⁺, W³⁺ and W⁶⁰⁺).

  • screening_window: momenta where partial screening applies. With :excitation_energy the bound-electron parts of charge state j (the Bethe term of ν_s, the g_j(p) of ν_D) are weighted by a smooth step in the kinetic energy (γ - 1) mₑc², 0 below screening_window_start · I_j and 1 above screening_window_end · I_j, with I_j the mean excitation energy. Below the window the frequencies are those of complete screening. The default factors 1 and 4 (a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package) place the window of Ar⁺ and Ar²⁺ at p ≈ 0.03 to 0.06 (Hesslow et al. PRL 2017 and JPP 2019). :dream applies partial screening at all momenta, as DREAM does. The weight depends only on p and I_j, so it holds unchanged when the densities become unknowns.

  • bremsstrahlung: :stopping_power, the radiative energy loss of an electron on the nuclei of all charge states as a slowing down (DREAM's BREMSSTRAHLUNG_MODE_STOPPING_POWER): the non-screened formula 4BN of Koch and Motz (Rev. Mod. Phys. 31, 920, 1959), ν_s = n Z² α r₀² c φ_rad(p)/β per charge state (bremsstrahlung_cross_section), on the nuclei only, as in DREAM: there is no electron-electron bremsstrahlung, so the stopping lies below ESTAR for hydrogen by 1.21 at 212 keV and 1.55 at 1.11 MeV, tending to Z(Z + 1)/Z². It is the default (a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package). :neglect leaves the loss out, as DREAM does by default.

  • bremsstrahlung_diffusion: whether the energy diffusion ν_∥ = Θγν_s of mode = :full with screened_diffusion = :maxwellian takes the radiative stopping of bremsstrahlung. :none (the default, a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package): the radiative stopping is a pure drag, as in the model of M. Hoppe et al., Comput. Phys. Commun. 268, 108098 (2021), doi:10.1016/j.cpc.2021.108098, eqs. 8, 9, 14 and 15, so a Maxwellian at the temperature of the bulk loses its bremsstrahlung power, which tends to that of Gould's formula at low temperature (GOULD_BREMSSTRAHLUNG_PREFACTOR). :dream: the code of DREAM (src/Equations/ParallelDiffusionFrequency.cpp:59-66), whose ν_∥ rescales the whole ν_s, so the radiative drag gets a thermal energy diffusion, a Maxwellian at the temperature of the bulk radiates nothing, and a tail at Θ_eff loses 1 − Θ/Θ_eff of its bremsstrahlung. The DREAM comparisons with the stopping power on take :dream.

  • bremsstrahlung_constant: the constant term of the formula 4BN in the stopping power, :koch_motz (the default), 4/3 as in Koch and Motz, or :dream, 1 as DREAM's integer division writes it (src/Equations/SlowingDownFrequency.cpp:234), for the comparisons with DREAM that run with the stopping power on (bremsstrahlung_cross_section).

  • bound_friction: the slowing down on the bound electrons of each charge state with partial screening. :bethe (the default, DREAM's model) is the Bethe term of Hesslow et al. with the weight of screening_window. :ionization_split splits it into the energy loss of the ionization of the charge state, from the cross section of the kinetic ionization (ionization_cross_section), and the rest, the excitation, floored at zero:

    \[ν_j = e(p)\, ν_{\mathrm{ion},j} + \max\left(w_j(p)\, ν_{\mathrm{Bethe},j} − e(p)\, ν_{\mathrm{ion},j},\, 0\right), \qquad ν_{\mathrm{ion},j} = \frac{γ}{p^2} n_j σ_j(p) v \frac{I_j + ⟨E_s⟩_j}{m_e c^2},\]

    with the ionization energy I_j of NIST (ionization_energy), the mean energy of every secondary ⟨E_s⟩_j up to (E − I_j)/2 in the spectrum secondary_spectrum (no cut: every secondary joins the cold electrons) and the envelope e(p) of the hot region of the kinetic ionization. f then pays I_j + ⟨E_s⟩_j for each ionization it causes, also from the ionization potential up to the mean excitation energy, where the default screening_window gives no Bethe friction. Elements need a fit of the cross section, and with secondary_spectrum = :opal a width of the secondary spectrum (SECONDARY_SPECTRUM_WIDTHS, secondary_widths). With the momentum faces of each radius given to the model (CollisionModel(plasma, settings; faces), as set_fokker_planck_coefficients! does), the ionization part is tabulated once per momentum grid and charge state at the faces.

  • ionization_threshold: the factor of the thermal momentum p₀ = factor √(2T/mₑc²) at the threshold of the kinetic ionization, which the region of the KineticIonizationTerm of the system takes as well; 0 (the default) for a kinetic ionization over the whole grid. Below the threshold the ionization is that of the cold electrons, whose binding energy the cold electrons pay (RadiatedPowerTerm with kinetic_ionization), so f pays none there. The envelope e(p) of the faces is a cubic smooth step from (1 − ionization_threshold_width) p₀ to (1 + ionization_threshold_width) p₀, a numerical width that keeps the coefficients smooth when the threshold crosses a face. The default is 0.1 (a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package); DREAM has no counterpart. With screened_diffusion = :maxwellian the energy diffusion Θγν_s takes the split as well, as it takes the Bethe term.

  • secondary_widths: widths Ē [eV] of the secondary spectrum per element, added to SECONDARY_SPECTRUM_WIDTHS for :ionization_split.

  • secondary_spectrum: the spectrum of the secondaries of ⟨E_s⟩_j: :beb (the default, a choice of this package; see the docs page Choices of this package), the binary-encounter-Bethe spectrum of Kim and Rudd with the ionization energy of each charge state as its binding energy (beb_mean_secondary_energy), which needs no width; :opal, Opal et al. with the width of the neutral element for every charge state (mean_secondary_energy).

  • ionization_cross_section_form: the form of ionization_cross_section in the friction of bound_friction = :ionization_split: :chianti (the default), :dream or :bethe. A system pairs it with the ionization_cross_section_form of its KineticIonizationTerm, so f pays for the ionizations that term counts.

Runes2P.bremsstrahlung_cross_section — Method
bremsstrahlung_cross_section(p; constant=:koch_motz)

The radiative energy-loss cross section of an electron of momentum p (units of mₑc) on a bare nucleus, in units of α r₀² Z²: the formula 4BN of H. W. Koch and J. W. Motz, Rev. Mod. Phys. 31, 920 (1959), doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.31.920, without screening, as DREAM's src/Equations/SlowingDownFrequency.cpp:194-237. It tends to 16/3 for p → 0 (H. Bethe and W. Heitler, Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 146, 83 (1934), doi:10.1098/rspa.1934.0140) and to 4(ln 2γ − 1/3) for γ ≫ 1 (W. Heitler, The Quantum Theory of Radiation, 3rd ed., 1954).

DREAM's line 234 writes the constant 4/3 of the formula as an integer division, which is 1 in C++, so DREAM's stopping power is 6.3 to 6.5 % above this one for p ≤ 1, 3.1 % at p = 10 and 1.7 % at p = 100. Runes2P keeps 4/3; constant = :dream gives DREAM's value, for the comparisons with DREAM. DREAM returns 0 at p = 0; the limit is 16/3, and the slowing-down frequency is 0 there anyway.

Runes2P.mean_excitation_energy — Method
mean_excitation_energy(Z, Z0)

Mean excitation energy [eV] of charge state Z0 of element Z (DREAM SlowingDownFrequency::GetAtomicParameter). Up to Z = 18 the tabulated values of Sauer, Sabin and Oddershede (2018). For Z = 19 to 86 DREAM's extended table, a semi-empirical electron-density model (DREAM tools/Generate_MeanExcitationEnergy.py), which is not hydrogenic for one and two bound electrons: 5123.6 eV for K¹⁸⁺ against 14.99 Z² = 5411 eV. Beyond Z = 86 the analytic formula of Sauer, Sabin and Oddershede.

Runes2P.p3_slowing_down_frequency_at_zero — Method
p3_slowing_down_frequency_at_zero(model, ir)

lim_{p→0} p³ ν_s, which sets the particle flux through p = 0. As DREAM's SlowingDownFrequency::GetP3NuSAtZero, the value uses the prefactor 4π r₀² c in every mode. In :ultra_relativistic mode, where ν_s ∝ 1/p and the analytic limit is 0, it therefore returns the :superthermal value. The friction of that mode is the constant drift Aᵖ = -4π r₀² c lnΛ n, which brings electrons to p = 0 in a finite time, and DREAM's value lets them leave the grid there: the sink through p = 0 is 1/Δp₁² times the inflow through the first interior face at equal f, so the first cell absorbs. With the analytic limit the first cell would collect them without bound. DREAM runs the mode on a runaway grid from p = 0 (examples/init_f_re).

Runes2P.parallel_diffusion_frequency — Method
parallel_diffusion_frequency(model, ir, p)

Parallel (energy) diffusion frequency ν_∥ [s⁻¹], Dᵖᵖ = ν_∥; nonzero only in :full mode. With screened_diffusion = :maxwellian it is Θγ times the collisional part of ν_s, and with bremsstrahlung_diffusion = :dream times the whole ν_s (CollisionSettings).

Runes2P.slowing_down_frequency — Method
slowing_down_frequency(model, ir, p)

Slowing-down frequency ν_s [s⁻¹] at radius index ir and momentum p; the collisional friction is Aᵖ = -p ν_s. With bremsstrahlung = :stopping_power it adds, per charge state of density n and nuclear charge Z, n Z² α r₀² c φ(p) γ/p with the cross section φ of bremsstrahlung_cross_section, as DREAM's src/Equations/SlowingDownFrequency.cpp:204-207 and :236 (where DREAM's φ differs by its integer division, see there).
