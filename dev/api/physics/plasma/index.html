Runes2P.IonComposition — Type
IonComposition(ions)

The species of a plasma and their charge states, without densities: the names, the nuclear charges Z, and the components of each species in an unknown of the ion densities (ion_densities_unknown), where species s holds the charge states Z0 = 0, …, Z_s at the components species_components(composition, s), in the order of ions. ions is a list of IonSpecies, a PlasmaState, or pairs name => Z.

Runes2P.PlasmaState — Type
PlasmaState{T}

Background ("cold") plasma seen by the kinetic electrons at each radius: cold-electron temperature [eV] and density [m⁻³], ion species, the electric field [V/m] (DREAM's E_field, ⟨E·B⟩/√⟨B²⟩) and the minimum magnetic field on each flux surface [T]. cold_density_given records whether the cold-electron density was given, or is the free-electron density of the ions by default: a system that holds the electrons of the grids as unknowns refuses that default as the target of the e-e collisions.

Runes2P.atomic_element — Method
atomic_element(name)

The element of an ion species named name, whose atomic data the species takes (ADAS tables, ionization energies, cross sections, masses): the name itself, or for a label of the form "<element>_<label>" the part before the underscore. A label keeps two species of one element apart, such as the injected deuterium "D_inj" of DREAM's disruption example next to the background deuterium, which DREAM calls Dinj and evolves with an ion energy of its own (examples/Disruptions/generate.py:439).

Runes2P.PlasmaFromUnknowns — Type
PlasmaFromUnknowns(prescribed; temperature=:cold_electron_temperature,
                   density=:cold_electron_density, ions=nothing, temperature_floor=0.5)

A PlasmaSource whose cold-electron temperature [eV] and density [m⁻³] are the values of radial unknowns of the system at each radius, with the rest of the plasma (ions, fields) from prescribed, a PlasmaState, a function of time that returns one, or a PrescribedPlasma. density = nothing keeps the density of prescribed, which a PlasmaState built without cold_electron_density takes as the free-electron density: in a fully kinetic case the target of the e-e collisions then counts the hot and runaway electrons as well, where DREAM's target is the closure n_cold = n_free − n_hot − n_re. The values are taken without their derivative parts, so the coefficients of a term hold numbers. Their derivatives enter the Jacobian as columns of their own.

ions names the radial unknown of the densities of the ion charge states, whose components follow the species of prescribed in their order (IonComposition, ion_densities_unknown); the densities of the prescribed ions are then ignored. A plasma whose ions are unknowns takes its density from an unknown too: the free-electron density changes with the charge states, and the prescribed density would not follow it.

A temperature at or below temperature_floor [eV] raises a DomainError, which the Newton iteration reports as a failed solve and the stepper answers with a shorter step: a step that would end below the floor is refused. The default is 0.5 eV (DEFAULT_TEMPERATURE_FLOOR). DREAM has no floor, so a comparison that follows DREAM below 0.5 eV sets a lower value. The floor is also the lower limit of the temperature unknown in the Newton iteration, which damps an update that would cross it (LowerLimit, NewtonSolver). The numerical limit lies far below: the collision model needs the scaled Bessel functions of mₑc²/T, which fail above an argument of about 1e9, near 5e-4 eV.

Runes2P.PlasmaSource — Type
PlasmaSource

Source of the PlasmaState that the coefficients of a term are built from. plasma_values(source, u, layout, t) gives the state at the state vector u of the system and the time t [s]. PrescribedPlasma is a plasma prescribed as a function of time, PlasmaFromUnknowns takes the cold-electron temperature and density from unknowns of the system.

Runes2P.PrescribedPlasma — Type
PrescribedPlasma(plasma_at)

A PlasmaSource prescribed in time: plasma_at is a PlasmaState or a function of the time [s] that returns one. The state of the last time is kept, since the evaluations of a term at one time share it. A PlasmaState given directly is constant for the whole run, and a term reads it once.

Runes2P.changed_plasma! — Method
changed_plasma!(key::PlasmaKey, source::PlasmaSource, u, layout, t; electric_field=true)

The plasma of source at the state u and the time t when its values differ from those of key (update_key!, which stores them), and nothing when they do not. A prescribed source is compared once per time, so the evaluations of a term at one time cost no comparison. ion_state is the state the ion densities of a PlasmaFromUnknowns are taken from, the start of the step for a term that lags them.

source
Runes2P.dual_plasma — Function
dual_plasma(plasma::PlasmaState, with_density::Bool, with_ions::Bool=false)

The plasma with the cold-electron temperature as a dual number of partial 1 at every radius, with with_density the cold-electron density as partial 2, and with with_ions the densities of the charge states as the partials after them, in the order of the components of the ion densities (IonComposition); the rest holds zero partials. The coefficients at a radius depend only on the plasma of that radius, so partial k of a coefficient at radius ir is its derivative with respect to plasma scalar k at ir, for all radii in one evaluation. plasma_columns gives the entries of the state of the partials.

The plasma with the densities of its ion charge states taken from the radial unknown name of the state u, in the number type of u: a term that evaluates its residual with dual numbers keeps their derivative parts, so its Jacobian holds the columns of the ion densities.
