Radial transport

src/operators/radial_transport.jl

Public

Runes2P.add_rechester_rosenbluth_diffusion! — Method
add_rechester_rosenbluth_diffusion!(op, delta_b_over_b; major_radius, safety_factor=1)

Radial diffusion of electrons along stochastic field lines (Rechester and Rosenbluth, Phys. Rev. Lett. 40, 38, 1978), added to op.diffusion_r_r:

\[D^{rr} = \pi q R_0 \left(\frac{\delta B}{B}\right)^2 |v_\parallel|, \qquad v_\parallel = \frac{c p \xi}{\gamma}.\]

The form with v_∥ and the factor π is that of Papp et al. (arXiv:1504.05074, section 2) and, with q = 1, of Berger et al. (arXiv:2208.02117, section 2), the second from the DREAM group.

delta_b_over_b and the safety factor q are numbers, have one value per radial face (radii + 1, as DREAM's dBB on its radial flux grid) or are callables of r evaluated on the faces (radial_face_values), major_radius is R₀ in metres. The pitch of the coefficient is that of the pitch cells of the radial face (radial_face_overlap), which are the pitch cells of the grid when all radii share their pitch grid. A level of zero on a face leaves the face without transport.

DREAM (RechesterRosenbluthTransport.cpp) has q = 1 fixed and sets R₀ = 1 in cylindrical geometry, where its major radius is infinite, so that the level of the perturbation alone sets the coefficient. major_radius = 1 with the default safety factor reproduces it. A physical coefficient in a cylinder needs the major radius of the device, and DREAM needs dBB √R₀ for the same coefficient.

Runes2P.radial_particle_flux — Method
radial_particle_flux(op::AdvectionDiffusion, f, face)

Particles per second that cross the radial face face (between the radii face - 1 and face) towards larger radii, Σ 𝒱' Γʳ Δp Δξ over the momentum cells, in the normalization of total_particles: the content of the radii inside the face falls by this flux and by nothing else when only radial transport acts. Face radii + 1 is the edge of the plasma, where the flux is the loss of the plasma (zero with r_boundary = :zero_flux). The flux through the first face is zero.
