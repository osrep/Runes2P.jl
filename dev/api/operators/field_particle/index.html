Runes2P.FieldParticleOperator — Type
FieldParticleOperator(grid, plasma, settings=CollisionSettings();
                      modes=3, scheme=:exponential_fitting, pitch_rule=:cell_center)

Model field-particle term of the linearized electron-electron collisions, of rank modes per radius. It conserves parallel momentum exactly, and it reproduces the matrix elements of the Landau field-particle operator between the first modes Laguerre modes of the l = 1 part of f, as the improved Sugama operator does (Sugama et al., Phys. Plasmas 26 (2019), doi 10.1063/1.5115440):

C_fp(f) = Σ_ab g_a A_ab (ℓ_b · f),   ℓ_b = C_eeᵀ (m ∘ L_b),   g_a = f_M ℓ_a / Δ𝒱,

per radius, a, b = 0, …, modes - 1.

  • C_ee is the discrete electron_electron_operator with scheme.
  • m is the parallel momentum of each cell, m_ij = 𝒱'_ij Δp_i p_i w_j, with the pitch weights w_j of pitch_moment_weights(; pitch_rule).
  • L_b = L_b^{(3/2)}(x²) are the Laguerre polynomials of the Braginskii expansion, x² = (γ - 1)/Θ at the cold-electron temperature (L_0 = 1, L_1 = 5/2 - x²). ℓ_b · f is the rate at which C_ee changes the moment (m ∘ L_b) · f, so ℓ_0 · f is the momentum C_ee removes.
  • g_a is the Maxwellian f_M ∝ exp(-(γ-1)/Θ) times ℓ_a per unit phase-space volume Δ𝒱_ij = 𝒱'_ij Δp_i Δξ_j, made particle-free by subtracting its density times f_M. In the continuum limit g_a ∝ C_ee(p ξ L_a f_M): g_0 is the shifted Maxwellian of the model operator of Hirshman and Sigmar (1976), and g_1 its heat-flux counterpart.
  • A is fixed by the matrix elements T_bd = ℓ_b · φ_d of C_ee between the modes φ_d = p ξ L_d f_M: the field-particle elements become R_bd T_bd, with R the Landau ratios N^{bd}/M^{bd} for like species (R_0d = R_b0 = -1, R_11 = -27/59). Row 0 makes m · (C_ee f + C_fp f) = 0 for every f to round-off.

With modes = 1 this is the Hirshman-Sigmar (and Sugama 2009) momentum-restoring model. It is exact for a shifted Maxwellian and gives 0.5172 σL at Z = 1 against the Spitzer-Härm 0.5816 σL. The heat-flux mode closes most of the gap, the third mode the rest (test/test_field_particle.jl).

The ratios R are those of the nonrelativistic Landau operator, so the term is accurate for a nonrelativistic bulk (T ≪ mₑc²). The default pitch_rule = :cell_center (w_j = ξ_j Δξ_j) is the left eigenvector of the discrete pitch scattering, so the modes are exact first Legendre modes on the grid and the conductivity does not depend on the pitch resolution (to 1e-5). With :linear, the moment of current_density, it converges to the same value at second order in the pitch cell width. The normalization of f_M cancels. On a pitch grid symmetric about ξ = 0 the term vanishes on any f even in ξ. Needs collision mode :full, where the bulk is part of f, and cold electrons at every radius. The pitch grids may differ between radii, with the same cell counts at every radius. The momentum faces are the same at every radius, as AdvectionDiffusion requires.

Runes2P.FieldParticleTerm — Type
FieldParticleTerm(unknown, operator::FieldParticleOperator; plasma=nothing, settings=nothing)

The momentum-conserving field-particle term FieldParticleOperator applied to the kinetic unknown unknown, added to its own equation. It couples all cells of a radius, so it is :matrix_free, with a Jacobian of rank modes per radius (low_rank_jacobian) that the block preconditioner of GMRES and SparseLU take by a Woodbury update. Linear in f.

plasma is the source of the plasma that the functionals and the restoring shapes follow: a PlasmaSource, a PlasmaState or a function of time that returns one, as for the FokkerPlanckTerm. They are rebuilt with set_field_particle_coefficients! and settings (by default those the operator was built with) when the plasma values of a radius change. The Woodbury factors of the solvers are taken from low_rank_jacobian at each factorization, so they follow.

A plasma that reads unknowns (PlasmaFromUnknowns) is always taken at the state at the start of the step (prepare_step!), the lagged treatment of the FokkerPlanckTerm. The derivative of the term with respect to the temperature and the density at a radius is a dense column of all cells of that radius, which has no place in the low-rank Jacobian of the term.

Runes2P.electron_electron_operator — Method
electron_electron_operator(grid, plasma, settings; scheme=:exponential_fitting,
                           p_lower_boundary=:zero_flux, p_upper_boundary=:outflow)

Test-particle operator of the electron-electron collisions alone: slowing down, pitch-angle scattering and, in settings.mode = :full, energy diffusion Θγν_s, from the free electrons of plasma with the thermal factors of the mode (:full or :superthermal). Bound electrons and ions are left out. Both ends of the momentum grid have no flux (the friction points inwards at p_max, and the slowing down vanishes at p = 0), so the operator conserves particles. The coefficients take the number type of the plasma, so a plasma with derivative parts gives the derivatives of the operator.
