Comparison with DREAM

This page compares Runes2P with DREAM, the code whose design it follows (M. Hoppe, O. Embréus and T. Fülöp, Comput. Phys. Commun. 268, 108098 (2021), doi:10.1016/j.cpc.2021.108098). The comparison is with DREAM release v26.5 and its branch boltzmann-eqterm at commit 93d27e1, the builds of the Reference data. The file and line citations of DREAM's source refer to commit 93d27e1 unless they name another version.

Runes2P is cylindrical: a straight magnetic field, constant on each flux surface, so no trapping and no flux-surface averages (cylindrical_grid). Every toroidal feature of DREAM is therefore not implemented.

Run modes

Runes2P has no switch of the run mode. A run is an EquationSystem of the terms the caller chooses, so a mode is a choice of unknowns and terms.

What Runes2P has that DREAM does not

  • Exponential fitting, the default scheme of fokker_planck_operator (Positivity and flux limiters).
  • The flux limiter van Albada, scheme = :van_albada of AdvectionDiffusion.
  • A smooth momentum grid, tanh_p_faces.
  • A pitch grid per radius, with the same cell counts and momentum faces at all radii, and conservative radial fluxes between different pitch grids (PitchOverlap, radial_face_overlap).
  • A conservative flux between the hot-tail and the runaway grid by overlap cells, pitch_mapping = :overlap of MomentumInterfaceTerm.
  • The momentum-conserving field-particle term of the electron-electron collisions, FieldParticleTerm.
  • The conductivity of the discrete kinetic operator, kinetic_conductivity, as the correction of the ohmic current (kinetic = :discrete_operator of ConductivityModel).
  • The total energy of the electrons in the fully kinetic mode, total_energy_equation, which closes the energy of the cold electrons.
  • A smooth window of partial screening above the excitation energy, screening_window of CollisionSettings.
  • The friction of the ionization on $f$, bound_friction = :ionization_split of CollisionSettings.
  • The heating of the cold electrons by the secondaries of the kinetic ionization, SecondaryHeatingTerm.
  • The split of the ionization at the threshold of the kinetic ionization, with the fluid coefficient of the cold electrons below it (fluid_ionization_coefficient, kinetic_ionization of IonRateTerm).
  • CHIANTI's cross sections of the kinetic ionization continued by the relativistic Bethe form, and DREAM's form with the factor $1/β^2$ (form = :chianti, :bethe of ionization_cross_section).
  • The knock-on pitch integrated over each pair of momentum cells and averaged over the primary pitch cell (MollerPitchKernel).
  • An implicit knock-on term, treatment = :implicit of KnockOnTerm.
  • A knock-on cutoff per radius that follows the plasma during a run, KnockOnCutoffUpdate.
  • The extrapolated flux at $p_\mathrm{max}$ by DREAM's stated definition, p_upper_boundary = :extrapolated_flux_consistent.
  • Absorbing edges with $f = 0$ or $T = 0$ on the edge face: r_boundary = :absorbing of AdvectionDiffusion, edge = :absorbing of HeatTransportTerm.
  • A safety factor and a major radius in the Rechester–Rosenbluth coefficient (add_rechester_rosenbluth_diffusion!).
  • Lower limits per unknown, relative to a reference unknown or to the density of a species (LowerLimit), and the temperature floor of PlasmaFromUnknowns.
  • Tolerances of the charge states relative to the density of their species, group of Tolerance.
  • A step controller with an error estimate from a predictor, and variable-step BDF2 (StepController).
  • A backtracking line search of the Newton iteration, line_search of NewtonSolver.
  • Preconditioners of GMRES for systems coupled through the fluid unknowns: FluidSchurLU, RadiusBlockLU, RadiusBlockILU.
  • Exact Jacobians by automatic differentiation, the columns of the plasma unknowns included (jacobian!, jacobian_correction_product!).
  • A schedule of legs with prescribed steps or a controller (Leg, time_points).
  • Checkpoints and segments of a run; with a direct solver a resumed run repeats the uninterrupted one bit for bit (CheckpointSettings, read_output).
  • The system as an ODE function of SciML (ode_function).

Behaviour without an option for DREAM's

  • Newton's method converges on both the update and the scaled residual, except for the linear steps that linear_acceptance accepts on the update (NewtonSolver). DREAM converges on the update; with setCheckResidual on, its default, it warns when the residual is not converged (src/Solver/SolverNonLinear.cpp:305-310, py/DREAM/Settings/Solver.py:28).
  • A failed Newton step is reported as a status of NewtonResult, and the time stepper retries it with a shorter step.
  • Each charge state has a lower limit relative to the density of its species (ion_densities_unknown). DREAM's MaximalPhysicalStepLength damps $T_\mathrm{cold}$, $n_\mathrm{tot}$, $n_\mathrm{cold}$, where they are unknowns $W_\mathrm{cold}$, $W_i$ and the species densities $N_i$, and $\sum Z_0^2 n$ per radius; the charge states have no limit of their own (src/Solver/SolverNonLinear.cpp:441-508).
  • A lower limit shortens only steps towards the limit, never growth (LowerLimit).
  • The ion rows take the sign convention of every other row, so IonDiffusionTerm takes a positive coefficient (DREAM: src/Equations/Fluid/IonTransientTerm.cpp:78).
  • coronal_equilibrium multiplies the ratios of the rates in log space, where DREAM multiplies them directly (src/Settings/IonEquilibrium.cpp:100-137).
  • The derivatives of the ADAS coefficients are exact, without DREAM's floors in the Jacobian (adas_coefficient, IonRateTerm).
  • The Jacobian of ExternalAvalancheTerm holds every column of the rate, where DREAM's holds those of the field and of $n_\mathrm{tot}$.
  • The temperature column of ParticleSourceTerm holds the derivative of the normalization of its shape, which DREAM's column omits (src/Equations/Kinetic/ParticleSourceTerm.cpp).
  • The recombination gain of radiation = :recombination takes the ionization energy of the recombined state at every radius. DREAM's takes that of the recombining state from its second radial cell on (src/Equations/Fluid/RadiatedPowerTerm.cpp). test/test_adas_cache.jl checks the radiated power in coronal equilibrium, which detects this mismatch.
  • RechesterRosenbluthUpdate takes the level of the perturbation at the start of each step; DREAM interpolates its table linearly in time and takes the value at the end of the step.
  • StepDoubling reads the tolerances by unknown, for the acceptance and for the next step; DREAM's step update reads them by position in its list of non-trivial unknowns (GetErrorScale, src/ConvergenceChecker.cpp).
  • check_every of StepDoubling takes the steps between two checked steps at the current length, the documented intent of DREAM's checkevery. DREAM's code takes them from a stale start time, with other lengths and one step more (src/TimeStepper/TimeStepperAdaptive.cpp).

Extrapolated flux at the highest momentum

DREAM's BC_DPHI_CONST is defined by a constant $\mathrm{d}Φ/\mathrm{d}p$ at $p_\mathrm{max}$, with $Φ = \mathcal{V}' Γ^p$, the phase-space Jacobian $\mathcal{V}'$ times the momentum flux $Γ^p$. Its code (fvm/Equation/BoundaryConditions/PXiExternalLoss.cpp) shares the $\mathcal{V}'$ of the last interior face between the two terms of the extrapolation and multiplies both by the advection coefficient $A^p$ of that face. The interpolation and the diffusion of the second term are those of the face before it. p_upper_boundary = :extrapolated_flux of AdvectionDiffusion ports this code literally, and the comparisons with DREAM use it. :extrapolated_flux_consistent extrapolates with the $\mathcal{V}'$ and the $Γ^p$ of each face, as the definition states. test/test_pmax_boundary.jl checks both, and test/test_pmax_boundary_dream.jl compares the literal port with DREAM.

Approximate Jacobians

The residual of a FokkerPlanckTerm is always that of its scheme, and jacobian selects the stored block alone. :exact differentiates the limiter, :frozen_limiter is DREAM's AD_INTERP_JACOBIAN_LINEAR, and a linear scheme name gives that scheme's Jacobian (with_scheme). jacobian = :upwind with a central residual is DREAM's AD_INTERP_JACOBIAN_UPWIND, which makes some of DREAM's runs at high momentum resolution converge (DREAM issue #126). A term with an approximate block declares jacobian_kind(term) === :approximate. Newton's method on the stored matrix is then a quasi-Newton iteration, and with GMRES(operator = :exact) the stored block only preconditions the exact Newton operator (jacobian_correction_product!). test/test_jacobian_strategies.jl checks the modes.

Line search

DREAM's nonlinear solver (src/Solver/SolverNonLinear.cpp) damps the Newton step and has no line search. With line_search = true, NewtonSolver backtracks on $\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert F \rVert^2$ of the scaled residual $F$ with the Armijo condition. It starts from the step length that the lower limits allow. The default is off, DREAM's behaviour. test/test_time_stepping.jl checks it.
