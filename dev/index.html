Runes2P

A relativistic electron kinetic solver for runaway electrons in the style of DREAM: the Fokker-Planck equation of the electrons on momentum grids per radius, coupled to the fluid quantities of a cylindrical plasma and solved implicitly.

source

Runes2P solves the relativistic Fokker-Planck equation of the electrons of a cylindrical plasma together with the fluid quantities that couple to it. The unknowns are laid out by an UnknownLayout: distribution functions on a KineticGrid, whose momentum grid may differ between radii, radial profiles and scalars. Each unknown has an Equation built from EquationTerms. The equations form an EquationSystem, $M \, \mathrm{d}u/\mathrm{d}t = \mathrm{rhs}(u, t)$. integrate advances it with backward Euler steps, or BDF2 steps with StepController, each solved by Newton's method (NewtonSolver), and write_output writes the result.

Installation

Runes2P is added from its repository, as described on the page Getting started.

Three parts are package extensions, enabled by loading their packages next to Runes2P (see Extensions):

  • HDF5: the output files and checkpoints (write_output, read_output),
  • SciMLBase: the SciML form of an equation system (ode_function),
  • Interpolations: interpolators of Interpolations.jl as radial profiles.

Questions and bug reports go to the issue tracker of the repository, https://github.com/osrep/Runes2P.jl/issues.
