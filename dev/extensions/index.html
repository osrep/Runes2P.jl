Runes2PHDF5Ext.Runes2PHDF5Ext — Module

HDF5 files of Runes2P: the output of a TimeSolution, the checkpoints of a run and their readers. Loaded when HDF5 is, so that Runes2P does not depend on HDF5.

Runes2PInterpolationsExt.Runes2PInterpolationsExt — Module

Interpolators of Interpolations.jl as radial profiles: evaluated on the points of the radial grid, and recorded by their points, the values there, the method and the rule beyond the last point, from which the run can be rebuilt.

source
Runes2PSciMLBaseExt.Runes2PSciMLBaseExt — Module

SciML form of an EquationSystem: loaded when SciMLBase is, so that OrdinaryDiffEq and NonlinearSolve can drive Runes2P systems without making SciML a dependency of Runes2P.

Runes2P.ode_function — Method
ode_function(system::EquationSystem)

SciMLBase.ODEFunction of M du/dt = rhs(u, t): in-place right-hand side f(du, u, p, t), the mass matrix, and the Jacobian-vector product jvp(Jv, v, u, p, t) (stored sparse Jacobian plus matrix-free terms). The sparse Jacobian itself (jac, jac_prototype) is included only when it is the whole Jacobian, i.e. when the system has no matrix-free and no explicit terms, and no term stores an approximate block (a frozen limiter, or the Jacobian of another scheme). In the ODE form explicit terms are part of f but not of the Jacobian, which then only approximates ∂f/∂u (as DREAM's explicit knock-on term does). A system with a term that lags part of its residual to the start of the step (lags_in_ode_form) gets neither jac nor jvp: in the ODE form the start of the step is the state itself, and the stored Jacobian leaves out the derivative of the lagged part. The solver then takes its own derivatives. The parameter p is ignored.

Cost: jvp assembles the whole stored Jacobian at u on every call (one AD pass per color, about 23 operator applications for the Fokker-Planck stencil) before the product. Fine for occasional products; a Krylov solver driven through SciML should reuse an assembled Jacobian instead.
