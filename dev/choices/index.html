Choices of this package

A choice of this package is a value or a default that neither DREAM's examples nor a cited reference fixes. The code marks each one where it is set and points to this page. Where a reason is stated, the column Notes gives it. Where DREAM behaves differently, the option that reproduces DREAM is named below the table.

Kinetic operator

ChoiceValueNotes
synchrotron loss of the Fokker–Planck operatorsynchrotron = true (add_synchrotron!)chosen for accuracy
interpolation scheme of fokker_planck_operator in p and ξscheme = :exponential_fitting (AdvectionDiffusion)chosen for performance and positivity
lag of the plasma of a FokkerPlanckTerm that reads unknownslagged = :ions-
boundary of fokker_planck_operator at p_maxp_upper_boundary = :f_zero, DREAM's BC_F_0; DREAM's default is BC_PHI_CONST (:constant_flux)-

The synchrotron default holds for fokker_planck_operator, set_fokker_planck_coefficients! and a FokkerPlanckTerm that rebuilds an operator built otherwise. DREAM leaves the synchrotron loss out by default, which synchrotron = false reproduces. The properties of exponential fitting are on the page Positivity and flux limiters. DREAM's default is the central interpolation, scheme = :central. With lagged = :ions the charge-state densities enter the collisions of f at the start of the step, and the stored Jacobian has no columns of them. The lag makes the term first order in time under BDF2, and ode_function passes no Jacobian for a system with such a term (lags_in_ode_form).

Collision frequencies

The rows are fields of CollisionSettings.

ChoiceValue
window of partial screening, in units of the mean excitation energy $I_j$screening_window_start = 1, screening_window_end = 4 (with screening_window = :excitation_energy)
radiative stopping on the nucleibremsstrahlung = :stopping_power (bremsstrahlung_cross_section)
energy diffusion of the radiative stoppingbremsstrahlung_diffusion = :none
width of the threshold of the kinetic ionization in the frictionionization_threshold_width = 0.1

The partial screening is that of L. Hesslow et al., Journal of Plasma Physics 84, 905840605 (2018), doi:10.1017/S0022377818001113. The bound-electron parts of charge state $j$ enter through a smooth step in the kinetic energy from $I_j$ to $4 I_j$, and below it the screening is complete. screening_window = :dream applies partial screening at all momenta, as DREAM does. bremsstrahlung = :neglect leaves the radiative stopping out, as DREAM does by default. With bremsstrahlung_diffusion = :none the radiative stopping is a pure drag, as in the model of M. Hoppe et al., Computer Physics Communications 268, 108098 (2021), doi:10.1016/j.cpc.2021.108098, eqs. 8, 9, 14 and 15. bremsstrahlung_diffusion = :dream gives the radiative drag a thermal energy diffusion $\Theta \gamma \nu_s$, as DREAM does. With a threshold factor ionization_threshold above zero, the kinetic ionization starts at $p_0 =$ ionization_threshold $\sqrt{2T/m_e c^2}$. Its weight in the friction rises in a cubic smooth step from $(1 - w) p_0$ to $(1 + w) p_0$, with $w =$ ionization_threshold_width, a numerical width without a counterpart in DREAM.

Kinetic ionization

ChoiceValue
cross sections of ionization_cross_section, KineticIonizationTerm and the friction of CollisionSettingsform = :chianti, cross_section_form = :chianti, ionization_cross_section_form = :chianti (chianti_cross_section)
energy above which CHIANTI's cross section follows the relativistic Bethe formCHIANTI_MATCHING_ENERGY = 100 keV
factor of the window of the weight between the continued fit and the Bethe formCHIANTI_MATCHING_WIDTH = 2
weight of the relativistic rise of each shell and transition of chianti_cross_section$1 - 1/u$, $u = \beta^2/\beta_I^2$
spectrum of the secondaries of SecondaryHeatingTerm, ionization_energy_flows and the friction of CollisionSettingsspectrum = :beb, secondary_spectrum = :beb (beb_mean_secondary_energy)
smallest threshold factor of the split of the ionization with a fluid termMINIMUM_SPLIT_THRESHOLD_FACTORS: 7 for :chianti, 5 for :dream and :bethe

The cross sections are the fits of the CHIANTI database, cited on Home, whose ionization data are those of K. P. Dere, Astronomy and Astrophysics 466, 771 (2007), doi:10.1051/0004-6361:20066728. These fits are nonrelativistic. chianti_cross_section evaluates them at the speed of the electron and adds the relativistic rise. Its weight starts at zero at the threshold of each shell, with $\beta_I$ the speed of an electron at the threshold energy. Above the matching energy $E_m =$ CHIANTI_MATCHING_ENERGY the cross section is the relativistic Bethe form, joined to the continued fit by a cubic weight in $\ln E$ from $E_m/2$ to $2 E_m$. The elements without CHIANTI data in CHIANTI_IONIZATION_FITS keep DREAM's form, and form = :dream takes DREAM's form for every element. The binary-encounter-Bethe spectrum of Y.-K. Kim and M. E. Rudd, Physical Review A 50, 3954 (1994), doi:10.1103/PhysRevA.50.3954, takes the ionization energy of a charge state as its binding energy. spectrum = :opal takes the spectrum of C. B. Opal, W. K. Peterson and E. C. Beaty, The Journal of Chemical Physics 55, 4100 (1971), doi:10.1063/1.1676707. It takes the width of the neutral element in SECONDARY_SPECTRUM_WIDTHS for every charge state. The fluid coefficient of the split (fluid_ionization_coefficient) has no floor, so with a Maxwellian f the kinetic and fluid rates add up to the ADAS rate. At a threshold factor below that of its form, IonRateTerm and RadiatedPowerTerm refuse the split. The factor 5 is the hot-region threshold of DREAM's disruption example, and the default hot region of ThermalMomentum takes 7 for every form.

Atomic data and radiation

ChoiceValueNotes
continuation of the ADAS coefficients outside the tables, in adas_coefficient, IonRateTerm and RadiatedPowerTermextrapolation = :dreamthe default behaviour matches DREAM, a code used by researchers in the field
radiation mode of RadiatedPowerTermradiation = :recombination-

Inside the tables the interpolation is bicubic in $\log_{10}$ of the coefficient over $\log_{10} n$ and $\log_{10} T$. extrapolation = :dream continues the bilinear interpolation of $\log_{10}$ of the coefficient in $\log_{10} n$ and $\log_{10} T$ from the edge cell, as DREAM's ADASRateInterpolator does. extrapolation = :clamp_density clamps the density to the table and extrapolates in $T$ alone; at low densities the coefficients are those of the lowest tabulated density, near the coronal limit. The radiation takes the ADAS classes PLT (line radiation) and PRB (recombination radiation and bremsstrahlung). radiation = :recombination takes PLT + PRB and accounts for the binding energy of every ionization and recombination. radiation = :dream is DREAM's default: PLT, the ionization loss and the bremsstrahlung of R. J. Gould, The Astrophysical Journal 238, 1026 (1980), doi:10.1086/158068. The erratum is The Astrophysical Journal 243, 677 (1981), doi:10.1086/158634. It reproduces DREAM's radiation with bremsstrahlung_corrections = :dream, which takes DREAM's coefficients of Gould's corrections.

Plasma from the unknowns

ChoiceValue
floor of a temperature unknownDEFAULT_TEMPERATURE_FLOOR = 0.5 eV

The floor is the default of PlasmaFromUnknowns and of fluid_temperature_floors. DREAM has no floor, so a comparison that follows DREAM below 0.5 eV sets a lower value.

Grids

ChoiceValue
pitch grid of runaway_gridbiuniform_theta_xi_faces(20, 12, 0.9): 12 cells uniform in $\theta \in [0, 0.9]$ rad and 8 up to $\pi$

The cells are finest near $\xi = 1$, the direction of the runaway beam. The pitch grid of the runaway grid is the same at all radii and independent of that of the hot-tail grid (Grids).

Knock-on cutoff

ChoiceValue
rule of cutoff_target$W_\mathrm{cut} = \sqrt{T W_c}$ where the critical momentum is finite and $W_c > T$, else no_ceiling_factor times $T$
factor of the cutoff without a thresholdno_ceiling_factor = 10
placement of KnockOnCutoffUpdateplacement = :energy_midpoint

$W_\mathrm{cut}$ is the kinetic energy at the cutoff and $W_c$ that at the critical momentum $p_c$, by default from total_critical_momentum. KnockOnCutoffUpdate applies the rule at the start of the run and after every accepted step, at every radius (per_radius = true). In DREAM the cutoff (pCutAvalanche) is fixed within a run and changes only between restarts. The cutoff is a face of the knock-on grid, kept while the target lies between its neighbouring faces (choose_cutoff_face).

Linear solvers

ChoiceValue
restart length of GMRESrestart_length = 50

When a step needs more Krylov iterations than the restart length, restarted GMRES can stagnate, and the step ends with :linear_solve_failed. The Krylov basis holds restart_length vectors.

Examples

The parameter values of the scripts of examples/ come from DREAM's examples or are choices of this package, cited in each script.

ChoiceValue
hot-tail grid of the switch grid=biuniform_270 (examples/common.jl)270 momentum cells to $p = 2.5$ with 70 below $p = 0.07$ (biuniform_p_faces); 15 pitch cells with 10 within 0.5 rad of $\xi = 1$ (biuniform_theta_xi_faces)

The grid=biuniform_270 preset is a fully kinetic hot-tail grid. The plasma of examples/cooling.jl is that of the reference case reference/dream/cases/cooling_positivity.py, and its radial values come from DREAM's SPI example.

Reference cases

The reference case reference/dream/cases/disruption_kinetic.py runs the disruption chain of DREAM's examples/Disruptions/generate.py with the parameters of examples/Disruptions/ASDEXU.py.

ChoiceValue
scenario of the disruption chain--scenario 1, the full-conversion scenario
elongation of the radial grid--elongation 1, a cylinder without shaping

The DREAM paper runs scenarios 1 and 4, and generate.py runs scenario 0 by default, which --scenario 0 reproduces. The cylinder of Runes2P has no shaping, so the case departs from the elongation of ASDEXU.py, linear from 1.0 to 1.15 in the radius, which --elongation asdexu reproduces.
